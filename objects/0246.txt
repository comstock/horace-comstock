1-22-43

[Grider Field
Pine Bluff
Ark.]

Dear Folks.

My present address is as follows:

A/C H.N. Comstock 43-G
Flight 7 Barracks 1
Grider Field
Pine Bluff, Arkansas

I am now flying. I have had 1 hr and 20 min of dual instruction and needless to say I love it. I have never done anything in my life that I like as well as I do this. From now on I'll take 1 hr. a day of dual up to 8 1/2 hrs. then I solo and from then on I fly alone except for an occaisional check ride. I have 2 hour classes in the morning and I am  on the flying line all afternoon.

This place was formerly a civilain aeronautic school and has been taken over by the A.A.F. It is more like a country club on a high class colledge than it is like and army training depot.
