From all I can find out the allies are giving the axis particular hell on all quarters. For that I am also sincerely grateful. I might possibly get in on the mopping up but that will probably be about all. Churchill predicts the fall of the Axis in Europe within 6 months and we say less. Time will tell.

I must stop now and get some badly needed sleep.

Your loving son and brother

Horace

P.S. Take good care of mine little wife please.
