April 18, 1943

[Basic Flying School
Coffeyville Kansas]

Dear Dad.

I had my 20 hrs check ride today and I think I passed it. I'll know for sure tomorrow. It won't make a whale of a lot of difference one way or another because once you solo in Basic you are pretty sure of getting through if your instructor is on your side and I have 30 hrs about 16 of which is solo and I have another instructor who I like much better than Lt. Hodges. Lt. Hodges was transferred to another squadron for the time being. I hope they keep him. He is a good instructor but too high strung for me to get along with. Everytime he cussed me I wanted to tell him I could fly a lot better with less static in the backseat but that wouldn't do.

You spoke of big ships in Bangor and Dad I saw some really bign ships over the weekend. A formations
