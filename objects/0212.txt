Oct. 12, 1942
GA.A.C.C.

[Army Air Forces
Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio, Texas]

Dear Dad

Thought I'd drop you a line and let you know how I'm getting along. I don't beleive I shall ever be sorry I made this choice. I am now in the finest branch of the service that there is. It is where the cream of American youth is because the physical is so rigid adn the mental exams are so tough.

The boys I have met so far have been the finest generally speaking I have ever known. A large percent of them are colledge [college] graduates.
