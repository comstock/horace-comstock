12-25-1942

[Group XIII
Army Air Force Pre-Flight School (Pilot_
San Antonio Aviation Cadet Center]

Dear Mom.

I got your long letter yesterday and will attempt to answer it tonight. I have just finished writing to Rene and Trafton and also to Elaine. You said that this would probably seem like a strange Christmas to me. To be perfectly honest it doesn't even resemble Christmas. I am sitting in my room in my underwear and all the windows are up. There is a soft balmy breeze blowing through and it is indeed hard to realize that somewhere there is a cold, white, world where people are singing Christmas chorales. Down here the grass is green and the sky is clear and it is like June in Maine.
