1-27-43

[Grider Field
Pine Bluff
Ark.]

Dear Dad.

I have had less time to myself since I arrived at Pine Bluff than in Preflight if that is possible. I am now tudying some real subjects such as aircraft engines, theory of flight, hydraulic systems etc. I fly about 40 minutes a day, weather permitting. I'm grounded today because of snow and sleet. This is the first time the army ever gave me something to do that I really enjoy. I am crazy about it and I can't get enough of it. I honestly thought I had been putting forth quite an effort in preflight but now I realize that I didn't ever know what is meant by effort. I am studying harder, living by stricter rules and accomplishing more per unit of time than I ever did before in my life. The ground school subjects are hard and deep but good staff and we have to know it. They are pushing us
