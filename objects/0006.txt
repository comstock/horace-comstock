1-11-43

[GROUP XIII
Army Air Force Pre-Flight School (Pilot)
San Antonio Aviation Cadet Center]

My Dear Floks

Tonight I am a little blue and discouraged. You see I have planned and planned and I can't see any way that Elaine can join me.

I haven't told you many of my troubles so for since I became a cadet but I've got to tell someone and you're elected.

Today I had another payday. I received a sum total of $5.07. Last month I got $21.22. Next month at this rate I'll owe our government something like $10.00. I've been to personel 4 times and I can't seem to get any satisfaction out of them. However I am going again tomorrow and see if I can wring some out. Since I have been a cadet, that is since the 6th of October[,] I have received all of $74.49. Ihave sent $50.00 of this to Elaine so you can see what an uproariously good time I have been having.
