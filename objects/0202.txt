There were 27 applicants for appointments to Air Cadet and only 6 of us made the grade. I am proud that I am one of those fortunate individuals.

A box of your home cooking would be very acceptable. In fact I am waiting with baited breath.

Some of the boys have received packages of that type from home and they all seem to arrive in good condition. Send me a thick chocolate cake and I'll be very very happy. Honestly I haven't had a piece of chocolate cake for so long that I've probably forgotten what it tastes like.

Tell Faith and Eulalie that their present arrived in good shape and is still under the process of being highly enjoyed. I also got the photo album and I've been staring at those pictures for hours on end -- I'll be very glad when you send me more to go in the case.
