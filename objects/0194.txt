It might interest you to know that I am writing this while sitting on the edge of Medicine bluff no. 1. There is about a 200 ft. drop directly in front of me and I can see many miles in both directions. This morning when I came out to wash there were about 15 deer outlined against the rising sun on this very bluff. Seasonal bucks adn some does and fawns. Part of Ft. Sill is in a game reservation so wild like is quite plentiful. You can't shoot deer anywhere
