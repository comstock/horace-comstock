May 20, 1943

[Basic Flying School
Coffeyville, Kansas]

Well this rainy spell is really turning into a first-class flood. This evening we spent out time ferrying the men in the engineer's corp. over to tow to work on the levee. You see we are stranded out here at C.A.A.F and the east side of Coffeyville near the river has been evacuated. This is the tenth day of allmost continuous rain. Everywhere it is mud, mud, mud! I haven't seen Elaine since Sunday night. I can always talk to her on the phone though and that is a whole lot better than being separated by 3000 miles.

I found out yesterday that I was going to single engine advance at Eagle Pass Texas. Eagle
