5-20-1943

[Basic Flying School
Coffeyville, Kansas]



I'm not over it yet. I have covered the most questionable part and should certainly finish now.

I haven't heard much from your next of the woods lately. I don't know whether my own mail is getting through or not. The tracks are washed out all around us. I guess if we leave here they'll have to fly us out.

I'm going to try to write to Rene and few others that I owe letters to so I guess I'll close this and get started or I won't have time.

Elaine and I are well and happy. We hope that all you folks are well too. Loads of love to all of you.

Horace
