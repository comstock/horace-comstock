Elaine has gone to Keene N.H. and I think she intends to settle down there in an apartment if she possibly can. She said she had a lovely letter from you and that she'd answer it as soon as possible. She writes regular book lenth novel's to me almost daily. It must be kind of discouraging for her because some days I write and some days I can't. She knows I do the best I can and I think she's wonderful so I guess that's what keeps her going.

It is 11:30 and I must go to bed because I have a rough schedule tomorrow. Tell Dad, Eulalie, Grammy C. and everyone else that I'll try to get a personal letter off to all in the next few days.

All the love that there is to the dearest, sweetest, mom that any guy every had

Horace
