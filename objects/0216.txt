Nov. 7, 1942.

[San Antonio Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio, Texas]

Dear Folks

I bought a new Parker pen and as these southerners say I just a writtin' the hell out of it. I really enjoy writing with a good pen and this is a honey. I bought it in the post exchange for $5.25. It is listed at $9.90 so you can see we get quite a discount on some things. It is one of the new Parker Lifetime pens.

Well as for as I know I will start pre-flight school next Wednesday and from then on it is up to me. If I start next Wed. Nov. 11, 1942 I'll have a pair of silver wings by the 22nd of July, 1943. That is a long time but believe me I can do it and by that time unless I'm sadly mistaken the Nazi Regime will be a thing of the past and we'll be concentrating all our efforts on crushing the slant-eyed yellow-men.
