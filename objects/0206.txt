Sept 15, 1942

Dear Mom.

I've just finished a 6 page novel to Elaine so if this is an incoherent mess I know you'll understand. I've written on my knee, a rifle butt and even a mess kit so much that I honestly am beginning to doubt my ability to ever write a good letter again. I really don't believe I could do it.

My first Sgt. just came in and borrowed a buck off me. I have about $25.00 out but it is all good as gold so I don't worry. If I had known that I might get a furlough this month I wouldn't have lent any out. It is too late now. I'll get it all back payday if I am here and if not they'll send it to me.
