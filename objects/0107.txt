1-31-44

Dear Mom.

Got your letter yesterday and was glad to hear you are back home again. I know Dad was pretty lonesome without you. He said so in a letter he wrote to me. Don't you feel complimented after all these years?

I find N. Africa kinda lonesome without my spouse likewise. In fact I find N. Africa kind of a morbid spot of earth anyhow. I often wonder what these natives do when ther are no "Yankees" around to laugh and have fun. The more I see of these foriegn people the more I realize  what a blessing God bestowed on my when he had me born American! You asked what you could send me. Well, here it is. 1. A silk, rayon or pongee scarfe. 2. A pair of light but tough unlined gloves. 3. Some chocolate bars. That just about covers the things I'd like but can't buy over here. Oh yes, I forgot -- some medium weight wool socks. Perhaps it would be best to wait untill you get my new address before you send them. So much love to the grandest fellow anywhere.

Love
Horace
