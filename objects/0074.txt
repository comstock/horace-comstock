Nov. 7, 1943

[U.S. Army Air Forces
Dale Mabry Field
Tallahassee, Florida]

Dear Mom.

Look my dear and you shall behold the original and the only one of its kind in captivity, the crazy stupendous, collasal species of mankind more commonly termed by his fellow men as--"The Hot Rock".

Yes mom that is a picture of your eagle son. Note that dam devil grin and those laughing eyes and alone all don't oh please don't tell me you can't see that mustache. If there is one thing that hurts my feelings it is to have someone say--"What mustache?" I think I'll have it dyed. Every time I go up on a high-altitude I come down with a blond mustache. The son bleaches anything at 30,000 ft. and it is really hard to keep it dark when I do so much high altitude work.
