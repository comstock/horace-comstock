You bet your life it is fast. Sometimes I wish I could faint but I can't and I'll be damned if I'll quit because I can't take it. IF I fall out I'm going to fall out cold! That is the general spirit throughout the rankes and it is the phyiological effect that they want. In bayonent drill they taunt us to make us mad adn viscious. The do.

Gee that is a pretty morbid outlook. It isn't really so bad. I admit it is tough but I'm tougher.

If I have to go on maneuvers my mail will probably take a sudden stop. You don't have much time to write when you march all day and fight all night.

I can't seem to say anything cheerful so I guess I'd better sign off and hope Steve's letter gets here soon!

"Viva La Comando"

All my love
Horace

P.S. I'm dog tired and don't know what I'm saying. I love you all a great deal.
