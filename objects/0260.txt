March 31, 1943

[U.S. Air Forces]

Dear Mom.

Here I am in Kansas. Boy do I get around. This is cetainly a long throw from Preflight School. This is a big base and a brand new one. About all we have is a big field with concrete runways and a few hundred ships and our barracks are single story wooden affairs without many conveniences. However they feed us the finest menu I have ever encountered in the service. The tactical officers are grand and really put themselves out to make us enjoy our stay here. The whole personnel department just run around in circles trying to make us happy.

I fly from Training Squadron I and we are called "The Green Hornets." All our ships are painted green on the noses and silver bodies and wings. I have 4 1/2 hrs. time all dual.
