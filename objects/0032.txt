I got a long letter from Earnie the other day and he is in Kansas City learning the fine points of cargo flying. He's attached to the T.W.A. He hates it because what he had his heart set on was light bombardment work and combat. He'll be flying heavy cargo planes and that isn't his or my idea of what we want in this man's army. However someone has to do it and I suppose there is practically nothing he can do about it. That was his assignment and he'd have to stick with it.

I am now an upperclassman with only 4 weeks left in Basic. It doesn't seem possible that I could be only 3 months from my wings and a possible commision but it is true and it makes me feel quite proud of myself for having come so far.

I wish I could see all of you but as you know that won't be possible for some time
