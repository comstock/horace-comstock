July 14, 1943

[Aviation Cadet Club
Matagorda Island Bombing and Gunnery Range]

Dear Folks

As you can see I am at the island. It is a really good place. We fly out over the Gulf and it is really beautiful. Our gunnery consists of about 2 weeks of nothing but firing at aerial and ground targets. Inasmuch as I really like to shoot and that is all we do you can understand that I am having a pretty good time. The food is the best I have had. Some damned fine meals.
