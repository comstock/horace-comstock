Feb. 21, 1943

[Girder Field
Pine Bluff
Ark.]


Dear Mom.

I got your letter this morning and also one from Faith. Inasmuch as this is Sunday and for once not a day of duty I am going to strive to catch up on my mail and studies. They have added a new plane to our training. Once a week we get up at 4:00 in the morning and woke until 6:00 on the maintenance line. The reason is because they are short of help even if they do cover it up by calling it maintenance course. On the days that we have this added chore we do not however have any alerations in our regular schedule. All we do is lose a little sleep and wash and grease a few planes. I am not complaining mind you because all I can learn here, however [tninial] it may seem, I am glad to learn and it may prove valuable to me one day.
