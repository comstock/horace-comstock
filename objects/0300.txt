Aug. 18, 1943
Baton Rouge, LA

[Page 1]

Dear Folks,

I intended to write this tomorrow morning but Elaine insisted that I write it tonight and I guess it is a pretty good thing because I'll probably be so busy tomorrow that I won't have a chance to write anything. This won't be very encouraging news but I guess the army doesn't believe in that stuff anymore.

To begin with I know this was too good to be true. The 85th Bomb Group is leaving Harding Field and of course I am going

on anyone but me. That sounds kind of funny but I guess I don't know exactly what I am saying. I hate like the dickens to send her home but there isn't much else I can do right now. So if you make a place for her there at home for a while I'll have a great load off my shoulders in knowing that she has a swell place to stay and someone to love her rather than parking the poor kid in a strange city and only seeing her once in a couple of weeks perhaps.
