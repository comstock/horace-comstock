Feb. 19-1943

Girder Field
Pine Bluff
Ark.

Dear Dad.

I have a few spare minutes before call to quarters so I'll dash off a few lines to you.

I got the $25.00 and [???] to get a watch in town but American made watches are as scarce as hen's teeth out here. In fact I couldn't find any except one [???] which was for sale for $81.00 and that was pretty steep. They had quite a few Swiss watches in the P.X. so I ended up by buying one of them. It is a "Hastonette" 17 jewel and it keeps very good time so far. One of the other fellows bought one in San Antonio and he has had very good luck with his.
