April 25, 1943

[Aviation Cadet Club
Coffeyville, Kansas]

My Dearest.

Today I came into town for the teh express purpose of seeing Random Harvest and I certainly enjoyed it. It made me so damned blue and sick for the sight of you that it isn't even funny. I'm afraid I wasn't in any mood to write when I cam out but here I go. And to heck with my mood. Why in the devil can't you come out here. I am getting to the point where I don't know whether I am coming or going. I expect you and yest I know you haven't started or you would have wired or something. God knows I'm going nuts waiting for some word from you. I'd give my right arm to see you.
