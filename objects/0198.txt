August 23, 1942

[For Hill
Oklahoma]

Dear Folks.

I guess I'm slowing down on my letters but it really can't be helped. We've been on night problems, guard duty and just about everthing. Consequently my mail has piled up pretty much.

Today they took all of us out to Anaclarks Okla. There is a big Indian reservation there and they were having a yearly festival. We had quite a time. They put on tribal dances, war dances and all the trimmings. There was one little fellow 14 months old that stole the whole show. He got in everyones was and did all the dances. Everyone go his picture except me. When we started back to the trucks we realized a little late. We ran and got them just as the trucks were going out of sight down the street. So we had to hitch-hike back to camp.
