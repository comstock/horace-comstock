May 31, 1942
Camp Bowie Texas

[page 1]

Dear Folks

This past week has been a tough one. The sun is so hot you can feel your flesh drying up and hardening likes tar paper. I spend most of my time crawling around on my stomach learning how to advance on an enemy under cover.

We boys from Co I are a choice group. The usual procedure is 13 weeks of preliminary training then 70 days of maneuvers. We however are getting only 8 weeks of prelim - before maneuvers so you can see they're poking the old drill right to us.

On our regimental insignia there are inscribed latin words meaning "Always foreward". That is their motto, creed and stand on life. In this outfit there is no such word as retreat. They were part of the Old Rainbow Division.

When I have finished my preliminary training and been on 72 days of maneuvers. I'll have a furlough. I'll be home for about 10 days.

I am going to have some pictures taken of my in uniform and I'll send you one.

All my love
Horace
