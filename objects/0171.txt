June 30, 1942

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

Dear Folks

I have written Elaine and Mr. and Mrs. Phil Wood tonight so if this is short and sweet please forgive.

The main thing is that Steve's letter of reference. I can't take my exams untill I gets here and if it doesn't come soon it may be too late!

The 27th of July we start maneuvers from which there is no turning back. We won't come back to Camp Bowie. We are supposed to go to our embarkation point and await shipment. Where we might end up God only knows.

If possible get me a reference whether Steve's or not. I don't want to go on those maneuvers if I can help it.

They have really put the screws on down here. We drill all day and hike all night. Only 18 miles las night. 3 3/4 hours. Fast?
