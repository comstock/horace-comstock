Oct. 7, 1942

[Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio, Texas]

Dear Folks

My present address you allready know if you've received the form letter freom the office. It is swell down here. We may stay here at the classification Center for 6 to 8 weeks and we may get out in 10 days. I certainly hope so. (10 days I mean) It is grand here but I am anxious to get started on [???] training. The barracks are new and its food is superb. We have a few odd regulations such as addressing each other as mister and eating with only one hand but I love it.

You'll have to excuse the pencil because my things have not arrived yet.

Tomorrow we started taking physicals and they are really tough.
