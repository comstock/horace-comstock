We sleep on big innerspring mattresses and eat in a cafeteria. There are canteens in the hangers and a big canteen in the cafeteria. We are flying Fairchild P.T. 19A's. So far I have learned to fly straight and level, make right and left banks, climbing turns, glidign turns and figure S's.

So far I haven't had much spare time but just as long as they let me fly they have all my spare time to use as they like. It seems as if we were living 23 hours a day to fly 1 hour a day. In this course they don't waste any time. You have to learn the fundamentals in 8 1/2 hours and the entire course consists of 60 hrs. of flying time plus a final check hop (cross country). My instructor is a swell fellow about 28 I think. His name is Haralson and he is the type of fello that never get excited or raises his voice. He just puts it up to you in a plain matter of fact way and you give your best to him because you, like him, and want to fly.
