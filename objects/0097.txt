1-23-44

Dear Dad

Low and behold! another letter for you today and one from mother dated 1-9 and 1-12 respectively. That's what I call quick service. That "V" mail sure does the trick. Mom said that Wilma and George were in sort of an extended fog since the arrival of the young Hawkins and I can well understand that they might be. I can hardly make myself believe that I a real bonafide uncle. I guess I'll just have to get used to the fact same as the rest of you.

Imagine that when I return to the States I shall have to become accustomed to several changes in my favorite family. The last picture I saw of Eulalie gave me a bit of a lift as our "limey" friends say. Probably Bill and Faith will be big enough so that I'll have trouble bridging the gap of time since I last I saw them too.

I know, Dad, that you must often wonder where I am and what I'm doing. All I can say to ease your worry is that I have all the faith in the world in my equipment, my supervision, my flying mates and my God.

All my love to all.
Horace
