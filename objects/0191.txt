August 7, 1942

[Fort Hill
Oklahoma]

Dear Dad.

I'll start a little note to you here and I really don't know how far I'll get. We just got back into camp. We've been on a 4-day problem under artillery fire and we are scheduled to start on a motor march at 12:15. It is now about 11:45. It was really fun to work under artillery fire. We spent 4-days advancing over the Okla. countryside. We dug in 4 times. We'd be sitting in our fox holes and Boooom! The artillery would open up. Then the ground under us would tremble and another explosion would follow that would be the shell bursting.

One time they dropped some 75mm shells within 600 yds of us and you should have seen the doughboys disappear like magic.

Whoopee! I just answered mail call and got 2 letters. One from Ralston Purina and one from Elaine.
