Nov. 22, 1943

Dear Mom.

I am now in the 439th Fighter Pilot Replacement Squadron. They just call it 439th Fighter Squadron but it is a replacement Squadron from which pilots get they permanebt assignments. I have been here two days and I am allready on a shipping list. Most fellows wait about 10 days or two weeks. Since I got here I have beenb through physical exams and 3 or 4 processings and I expect to start drawing some more equipment very soon.

Oh damn! There isn't any sense in beating aroudn the bush. The chances are about 50-50 for overseas duty. I won't know what that shipping list contains until 15 minutes before I move out of here so where I go will remain and absolute secret. I have attended about 6 lectures but that all get them whether they are going over or not. I have known all along that it would happen this way and probably you knew too. I am still in hopes of getting time
