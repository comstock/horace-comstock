June 8 1942
Camp Bowie Texas

Dear Folks,

Today I got another shot in the arm. They don't take effect for about 2 hrs and then oh boy. I got you letter today and a letter from Galen Goodwin Life Insurance Co. and carton of Luckies from Dad.

I'll try to give you an idea of this place. It covers about 10 square miles and is very closely populated. I wouldn't even attempt to tell you how many men are under arms and in training here. From 6 in the morning to 6:00 at nite we're under military supervision. Lights out at 11:00. They tell me that the first month is the toughest and I haven't even started to weaken yet. Down here we drill and drill. Actually in two things--close order--or parade drill and open file -- or combat drill. Close order is kind of fun but combat drill is damned hard work. The sergent says, "Hit the dirt" and he means it. It really doesn't matter what type of cround -- rocky, sandy, dusty or mud. When that command rings out you and rifle and equipment go down!
