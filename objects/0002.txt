It was quite a thrill to sit up there in the blue and know that I was entirely dependent on my own knowledge and newly acquired skill to get myself down again all in one piece. I sweat a little and an pretty busy keeping everything as it should be when I am up alone but I have never been afraid of it as so many of the have. I guess it is more or less in my blood because I have come to believe that haven't really lived until you have flown. I have never found any sensation that can compare with it. I think I thrive on it too. I weight 150# and I don't have an ounce of fat on me. I have a scholastic average of 87% and the subjects are a lot tougher than they were in Preflight.
