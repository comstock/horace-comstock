August 29, 1942

[Fort Sill
Oklahoma]

Dear Folks.

I am now an Air Cadet. Thursday morning I went before the board of two majors and a captain known as the Air Cadet examining board and was accepted. In a few days my C.O. will receive a letter stating that I have been accepted and an transferred to the Eighth Corp. Service Command. I'll stay here untill I am called up for training then I'll go to Ft Sam Houston Texas for final examination and shipment to a training center. God alone knows where that might be. It might be in Bangor, Maine who knows. Earnie Bernier is in Maxwell Field, Alabama. I guess I told you before. Thoday I am bulger of the guard and I can't leave thje Co. street so I guess I'll spend the Sunda writing and trying to catch up on my correspondence.

Roberta Faufaw writes me the news of St. Johnsberry quite regularly which is very nice of her.
