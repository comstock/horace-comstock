12-25-42

[Group XIII
Army Air Force Pre-Flight School (Pilot)
San Antonio Aviation Cadet Center]

Dear Rene and Trafton

Well, honey, her it is Christmas Day and I am desperately striving to catch up on my correspondence and my studies.

First, I got you package and low and behold a Kaywoodie! Judas Priest gal you hit the nail righ on the article. I hardly ever have time to smoke a cigarette so have been smoking a pipe allmost entirely for the last month and a half. There is something chummy about a good pipe and your studies in the envening. The pipes they sell her on the post are a crummy lot ranging from 0.35 cents to $1.50 and don't exactly last. A really good pipe like a Kaywoodie is a rare treasure.
