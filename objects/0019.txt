It stops for a moment at the wind "T" to check time, traffic, wind and altimiter and then the 175 horses pound out a sound of power adn 109 sails off into the blue. It climbs to 4000 ft adn gets away from the field. Then follows a series of crazy antics that are called "the getting it out of your system process." You loop, roll, stall, spin and peel off. When fifteen minutes have passede you are ready to settle down to work and practice a few of the prescribed maneuvers. An hour os so later a tired Mr. Comstock piles of of 109 and walks back to the stage house to eagerly await his next solo assignment. Six hours goes by pretty damned fast in this manner.

I passed my first check ride yesterday so I am pretty happy about the whole setup. Out of the five students Mr Haslam has honly two passed and I was one of them.
