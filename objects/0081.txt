Flying acrobat or as the Navy down here calls us, I am one of Uncle Sam's slap-happy "Buzz Boys." One of our boys got into an argument with one of the Navy fliers in Savanah and when asked what was the matter with the Navy he replied, "you guys wear stuffed shirts and stiff hats an can't fly a kite. We wear soft shirts and sloppy hats and can fly any blank blank thing with wings on it." I thought he told him off very well. There is quite a lot of friendly animosity down here but we still fly circles around them and thy know it too.

That's all for now.
