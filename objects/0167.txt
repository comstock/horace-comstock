June 21, 1942

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

Dear Dad.

This is "Father's Day" and I am thinking of you. I don't mean that I don't think of you on other days but rather I am thinking especially of you today. I guess we don't appreciate a lot of things as much as we ought to. Sometiems we have to get a long ways away from them to really understand their value.

It is a pretty grand feeling to by in this army and yet know there is someone on the outside on whom you can rely for practically anything.

One thing that I, for one, want very badly is to make my Dad proud of me. Not just because I gave up a good job and a lot of dreams that I had to come into this mess and shoulder my share but because I took my share and did a really good job of it. Yes sir a good enough job so that I could know that my Dad would be able to point to me and say, "that's my son." with
