Dec. 29, 1943

Dear Folks.

A very great many things have happened to me in the past few weeks. Most of these are of such a nature as would get them cut out by my censor if I were to write them. Incidentally one of the things I have been doing censoring mail. I get awful sick of reading other peoples' mail. Some of the things I can tell you are that I was in Casablanca and I am not anymore. Down there we were under the command of an Infantry C.O. with Infantry all around us. We caught hell. I am sure glad to be back with the Air Corp.
