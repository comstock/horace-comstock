July 27, 1943

[Army Air Field
Eagle Pass, Texas]

It is going to take some time to compose this letter so it will probably be written on the installment plan.

First I want to thank you for the $100.00. I can't tell you the mixed emotions that bounced around in me when I found it in your letter. I always get a kind of tight feeling in my throat when I get a leeter from you but this was almost too much. Gee, Dad, it is the sweetest thing that ever happened to me. Elaine and I both want to thank you from the bottom of our hearts. Not Just for the money but the sentiment and the love and understanding that goes with it.

In two days it will be Horace N. Comstock 2nd Lt. A.A.F. (with wings). Yes I got commisioned and I made "Expert aerial
