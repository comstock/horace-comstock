910 Elizabeth St.

Dear Mom,

This has been my day off so I am writing from Elaine's room in town. I slept until 10:30 this morning and then got up and took some dry cleaning out and had my breakfast. After dinner Elaine and I took the camera and a carefully loaded film and went over to the park and took 6 of me. Then we took them downtown to be developed. They said we ccould have them Wednesday. I am going to send one set to you as soon as I get them. I have been
