I have studied math, physics, naval and aircraft identification, navigation, maps & charts, aerial photography, code, ground and air force organization, signal communications etc. It has been fun and a lot of hard work and I am sincerely glad that it is behind me. I am now one quarter of the way towards getting my bars and wings. It will seem pretty wonderful to wear them.

I got a letter from Earnie Bernis yesterday and he has 44 hours of fasei flying behind him. He is just as crazy about it as I am and he has given me a lot of courage and good advice.

I am going to write to Elaine now so I'll sign off. This is a poor excuse for a letter from a good soldier.

All my love to all of you
