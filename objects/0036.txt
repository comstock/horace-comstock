5-4-1943

[Basic Flying School
Coffeyville, Kansas]

That is the ship the "Flying Tiger" flew.

I have really got to get on the ball in this letter writing business because I owe so many now I'll never get caught up I guess. I guess I can take a little time off tonight and get caught up or make a start in that direction.

I have a pretty close buddy here named F.W. Linde who is from Penn. and he and I and his wife and Elaine are making plans for an apartment in our Advance school.

If it happens to work out the way we want it to we should have a very satisfactory 2 months. Of course it probably won't work out the way we've planned it but we can hope until something more definate occurs to make us believe that it can't. Elaine and I spend the happiest weekends doing
