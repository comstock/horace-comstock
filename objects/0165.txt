June 14 1942

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

Dear Mother.

This letter will be a mess because I just finished writing to Elaine and Rene but I know you'll be glad to get it anyways.

Saturday we went on a ten mile hike with rifles and equipment and it was 109 degrees in the shade -- that's hot. Out of 300 men, 125 walked it. I was one of them. Believe me I was tired and I ached in every joint. This hike was done without water. Picture that if you can. 175 men either fainted or got sore feet so they couldn't walk. Some of the boys that fainted are in the hospital and two are on the danger list. That is the kind of a killer this training can be. Fellows like that have more guts than sense I'd say. If I was in that condition I'd fall out.
