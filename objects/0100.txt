1-9-44

Dear Folks

N.Africa is showing no change. How are things in <aine. I am feeling great and have never ceased to marvel at the quality of the food over here. I am eating so well or betther than anything in my military career. I have said many times that I could really enjoy this trip if the government wasn't paying me for it. Regardless of how I got here, I still think it is equal to a couple of years in colledge. I have seen a great many things and been to many interesting places. When I get home again I won't know where to begin in telling you all of the things that have happened to me.

I would sure love to see the rock-bound coasts of dear old Maine right now. When this mess is all over I think I'll buy a cottage on the coast and spend the rest of my life digging clams. I wonder how Elaine would like that/

Time and paper grow short so I must say good night. Just wanted you to know I am well and still here in N. Africa. Loads of love to the finest family any guy ever had

Horace
