August 18, 1942

Dear Folks.

I have a bit of something to write this time for a change. Heretofore you have addressed my letters to Pvt. H.N. Comstock. Until further notice please address them as P.F.C (Private First Class) Horace N. Comstock. Yes I now have one stripe on my sleeve. It isn't much but it is a start. It puts me one jump ahead of the rest of the buck privates.
