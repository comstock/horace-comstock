12-12-42

[Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio, Texas]

I have made one very good friend since I came in here. His name is Vance Curtis and he was formerly a mining engineer out of Seattle Washington. His nickname is "Hard Rock". His is a great man for the wilds and he loves to fish and hunt. In fact his only ambition in life at present is to get the war over with so he can return to his beloved mountains and mines.
