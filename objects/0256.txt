March 23, 1943

Dear Folks,

Here is your pilot (I hope) in Kansas. Yes, I got pick of Arkansas so I decided to make a change. This is a new school and we are going to get the benefits of a lot of experiments. The last 4 classes here set a very good record and our officers are grand. I start flying tomorrow morning. Today drew my flying equipment. It is swell. I get a gabardine flying suit, gloves, light leather jacket and headset. The heavy winter equipment is kept on the line which is a relief. That means we don't have to carry it back an forth.

I haven't met my instructor yet but I know he wont be as grand a guy as Mr. Haralson was. He couldn't be! In the afternoon we get our first taste of Basic ground school and from the stack of 20 odd books we get that won't be any cinch. Every time I move I get rushed worse. We have to change
