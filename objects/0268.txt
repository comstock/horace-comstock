June 8, 1943

[Army Air Forces Advanced Flying School
Eagle Pass, Texas]

Dear Mom,

Gee I've been a louse about writing but I don't seem to find time to even pick my teeth. I guess it has been pretty much teh same every time. I changed stations.

The name of the island and penninsula when we get gunning is "Matagoods". I believe you came pretty close. WE spend about 18 days down there and one bunch of boys just go back. They say they had a good time. They spend one day lying around in the sun and swimming and fly the next day. They said it was the "life of Riley." I guess I could use a little "Riley."

When I look back on the past months I find it bard to beleive that I have done what I have. Out of the original 26 men
