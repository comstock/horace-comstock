1-14-44

Dear Dad.

Just a line to let you folks know I am all o.k. To be honest I feel better than I have felt for a long time. Perhaps it is because I took a hot bath and put on some clean clothes today. Then too it might be because I checked out in a new type of aircraft today. It is the finest plane of its type in the world. I was a little flabbergasted when they gave it to me but I love it.

There is very little of interest happening down here on the "Ice cream" front so I don't have a great deal to write about. I just finished writing my daily letter to my wife so I just kept on writing. Oh yes! I am nursing another mustache!

Everyone is doing it over here. You never saw so many "cookie dusters" and beards in one group of men in your life. Well I;m getting short on news and paper so I'll say good night and sign off for the present.

Love
Horace
