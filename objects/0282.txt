June 26, 1943

[Army Air Forces Advanced Flying School
Eagle Pass, Texas]

Dear Folks

Tonight I am O.D. (officer of the day) and it is no picnic. I can't figure out just why I had to get stuck with it on a weekend but there it is and I can't help it a single bit. The thing that makes me maddest is that I can't see Elaine this weekend. Ah well, that is the way things go in the army. It is now 11:30 and from 6:30 untill now they have kept me in a constant dither chasing everyone and everything from cadets to the colonel. The class of 43F graduated today and there were more blooming "shave-tails" running around here than you could shake a stick at. About 40% of them got combat outfits and the rest are going to B29 engineering schools and instructors school. We had a long talk with some officers that have just returned from combat.
