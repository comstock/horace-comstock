Dear Folks

I don't know when you'll get this as I am quite a ways further away from home. Im fact the farthest yet. The plane pictured on this stationary is a very good picture of the ships I am flying now. That is exactly what they look like.

Before I get off the track, this, Eagle Pass, is the most desolate, god-forsaken, dead, dusty, windy hole that God ever created. However we have the best officers and morale and general attitude I have ever encountered. The work here is the most interesting yet. We shoot skeet get aerial gunnery, fly at 50 ft. on cross country countries also fly them at 20,000 feet both extremes. We will get 2 weeks on an island out in the Gulf of Mexico where we will get all types of gunnery and lives like a bunch of vacationists. They say it is just like a vacation.
