Our schedule was pushed up some 10 days and I had to work like the devil to keep up with the pace they sent but I don't believe I skipped more that two days without writing to her. I have written about 6 letters in the past two days which is remarkable for any cadet. That is about the only relaxation I had since I came down here, writing to my wife. I have been into San Antonio 4 times since I came down here. Three times in Classification center and once over here in Preflight. There is very little a married man can do in a town of this type (typical Arrmy boom town. Dancing, Drinking and general carousing seem to constitute the pastimes here and I don't go for any of it so I stay out here at school and study. It netted me a final academic average of 92.67% for all my studies so I don't feel that wasted
