yesterday and found out I am getting only a potential payment of about $50.00. I had hoped to get it all and square up with Dad but you can't argue with the U.S. Army.

I was as damned glad to hear about George. I know just how he felt and believe me he has a good job. I'll bet he'll look like a million in a uniform. I'm glad for Rene too and I wish you'd send me her address as soon as you get it so you can. I know she and Trafton will be happy together. I know it is kind of tough on you folks to have us all so far away but we'll all be back when this mess is ironed out and we can live like humans again. In some ways I like the army and I know why some men can make it their life's work. I could nver make it mine because I'm not built that way but I realize and appreciate the value of the training I am getting.

Loads of love to all of you.

Horace
