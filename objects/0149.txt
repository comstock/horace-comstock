June 5, 1942

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

Dear Mother

I am getting quite slack in my writing to you. It isn't all my fault tho. In the past week I have been on 3 twelve mile hikes and 2 eighteen mile ones. I have also walked a guard post two nites. This is all besides my regulary duties.

Yesterday was the 4th of July and pay day. The whole camp got drunk and inasmuch as I don't participate I found it rather a dull day.

I got my letter from Steve and went over to see about "Air Cadet." I can take my test after the 18th of July. The Co. clerk, Sgt. Kindred is a damned good egg. He is helping me all he can.
