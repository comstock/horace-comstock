2-4-44

Dear Folks

At long last I am in my own outfit. I am finally permanently assigned to an outfit here in Italy. There is a grand bunch of fellows in this outfit and it is one of the oldest groups here if not the oldest. It has a very good record adn there is no doubt that they know what they are doing. It is certainly a lucky break for me to be a assigned to such. The censorship here is such that it is pretty difficult to know just what to write about. I know though that you will be waiting for every letter I send with no little anxiety so I will make an extreme effort to keep you constantly in touch with your wandering son. They say the mail service is pretty good so I expect out correspondence will move quite fast. Believe me I couldn't have done better  under any circumstances I am very satisfied.

Loads of love
Horace
