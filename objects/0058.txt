Sept. 24, 1943

[U.S. Army Air Forces
Waycross Army Air Base
Waycross, GA.]

Dear Folks.

Here I am again. I am sitting in the pilots room waiting for my next mission. I just cam down from a formation flight. You know I can't believe that I am flying these hot pursuit ships and only a year or so ago it was just a dream. If a year ago someone had taken me up and done half of the things that I do every time I go up I would probably have been scared stiff. Like this formation I fly now, they call it tactical or combat formation. We fly about 50 yrds apart 14 ships in 2 elements like so:

T
  T
      T
        T
