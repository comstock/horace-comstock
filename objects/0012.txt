Grider Field
Pine Bluff
Ark.

1-29-43

Dear Mom.

I got your letter today and I was tickled pink. They are the first I have received since my arrival here at Pine Bluff.

Today I completed five hours of dual instruction. My instructor told me to make my take-off and I did supposing all the time that he was following me on the controls. When I reached 2500 feet he spoke for the first time. "Mr. Comstock," says he, "Turn around and look at me." Of course I did and to my amazement his hands were on top of the windshield and his feet on the dash-board. So I took off and climbed to 2500 feet all by myself. Tomorrow I expect to start shooting landings. I have shot seven already but he follows me pretty closely on the controls. Today during my 42 minutes I made 7 stalls both power on and power off.
