By the time you receive this I'll probably be on my way to Basic. There are two possibilities, one is Coffeemills, Kansas and the other is Waco Texas. Waci is where Johnny Irving is stationed at present. If I should get to go there I'd probably see him a few times during his remaining 4 weeks.

He has only 4 weeks left before he becomes a lietenant. In some ways I envy him and in others I don't. I'd like to be that close to my wings and yet there is a lot of fun and work ahead of me before I can claim mine.
