The only advantage in getting high marks here is that if you wash out and have a high average you go to O.C.S. and I don't intend to wash out but if by some trick of fate I did I wouldn't much care where I went. I have everything I want that the army can give me here with my flying and I don't intend to loose it if I can possibly help it.

This is about all I have time for right now so I'll sign off. Tell Rene I'll write to her just as soon as I can find a spare moment. God knows I don't have many of them now and for the first time since I joined Uncle Sam's fighting forces I don't mind.

All my love
Your son

Horace
