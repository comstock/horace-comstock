I will leave here for Basic on the 20th of March providing of course I pass all my check-rides and don't flunk in ground school. Out of the original 317 men who came up here with me from S.A.A.C.C. there are about 175 men left. There is one satisfying thing about the hight rate of wash-outs. That is that you damn well know them you fly with, know their business or they wouldn't be flying. You also know when you get thos silver wings that you earned them. I have never worked so hard or tried so desperately to please everyone as I have since I came here to Pine Bluff. This is undoubtably the finest Primary school in the country and if I graduate I will have the finest training of its type behind me that money can buy.

Lots of love
Horace
