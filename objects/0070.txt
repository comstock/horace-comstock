Nov. 3, 1943

[United States Army Air Forces
Dale Mabry Field
Tallahassee, Florida]

Dear Dad.

Got your letter today adn as allways was very pleased. I really enjoy a letter from you. I know that mother writes all the news and you sometimes feel that your are just updating something she has allready written but ther is something about getting a letter from you that really give my morale a boost. You see I think you are about the swellest Dad that any guy ever had.

You say that know so little about my present life that you couldn't ask a sensible question about it. You're wrong. Dad, I can think of a million things I used to wonder about before I got into it mostly about being scared. I was scared too, at first.
