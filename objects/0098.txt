Feb. 15, 1944

Dear Dad.

This morning I had the best breakfast I have had in ages. I had a big bowl of hot Ralston and real cream and hot buttered Rye-Crisp and coffee. Don't ask me where it came from. I don't have a ghost of an idea. All I know is I went over to the mess tent and there it was. For the first time in a long time I said, "Vanchego! encore!" Vanchego is our Italian waiter. Things have been pretty quiet lately. About the only time we see "Jerry" is when he's going hell bent for election in the direction of his own lines. He seems to have a healthy respect for Spitfires! He dosn't have any desire to stay and play with us. You probably read a lot of stuff about what is going on over in this part of the world and know more about it than I do. It seems funny to fly over the territory every day and come back and read the local doughboy papers to find out what is actually happening. However, that is the way it is.

Well Dad, I am in excellent health and not at all unhappy alltho I would give a great deal to see Maine right now so I guess that just about fills this form up.

Lots of love
your son
Horace
