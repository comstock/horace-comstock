June 1943

[Army Air Forces Advanced Flying School
Eagle Pass, Texas]

Gee I'm not going to even know my family when I get home. I imagine Eulalie resembles Wilma somewhat but it has been so long since I have seen Wilma that I don't even know what that is like. Incidentally I go for the idea of being an uncle. I wonder if I'll have a niece or nephew. Poor George!

I expect Faith and Bill are as big as I am by now. It is funny to me. I look back on an interesting and extremely pleasant childhood and here I am, married man soon to fulfill one of the most important jobs in the future of my country, possibly of the world, as a flying officer in the A.A.F.

Yesterday our P40s came in. Twelve of them in stepped down V formations came over at about 30 feet off the tops of the barracks
