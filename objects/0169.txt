6-21-42

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

they have a loudspeaking system here in the Enlisted Men's Club and they were playing the piano and singing. Your son was crooning away to his heart's content when he noticed that the loudspeaker had picked up one voice in particular and it sounded pretty good and strangely familiar. He turned his head to see who it was and looked squarely into a "mike." The hostess had stuck the long handled "mike" out to about my ear and what I was enjoying turned out to be my own voice. We were singing "My Blue Heaven." I got quite a hand when I finished and promptly sneaked off to the cafeteria to busy myself in and ice cold coke. Who knows I may end up touring the country with Kate Smith or someone huh?
