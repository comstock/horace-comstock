May 13, 1942

[Basic Flying School
Coffeyville, Kansas]

Dear Mom

I have a few minutes left so I can start this and hope to finish it sometime tomorrow.

We have had a stretch of real bad weather down here so we haven't done much flying. In fact in the past six days I have gotten only about 2 hrs. We tried to finish up our night local flying tonight but the ceiling closed in and we had to quit. We were pretty well up but now we are getting pressed for time so we'll probably end up by flying Saturday night and Sunday which will shoot my weekend all to grass. Ah well such is the life of an A.A.F. pilot. Elaine comes out every evening and it is pretty wonderful. I wouldn't exactly say we were deleriously happy because we don't have time for much delerium
