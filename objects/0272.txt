June 13, 1943

[Army Air Forces Advanced Flying School
Eagle Pass, Texas]

Dear Mom

I just got back from Carrizo Springs and a very enjoyable weekend with my wife. We went swimming this afternoon. I got a little burn on my shoulders but it isn't bad enough to be painful.

Tomorrow I start buying my officer's uniform. I have a $250.00 charge account at the tailor shop but I think I can get away with about $175.00 for the time being. When I become a flying officer I'll collect $327.00 per month. I don't know how much of it I can actually get but it is sure going to be a heck of a lot better than skimping along the way we are now.
