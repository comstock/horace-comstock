I guess I'll have to [???] someone till I get some action.

St. Louis is sending me the "Monday Morning Messages" regularly now and it seems pretty nice of them. I will have to inform them of my change in address tomorrow.

They reorganized the Co. again and this time made me a dispatch carrier from H.Q. to the first platoon. I haven't had time to find out just what it means but from what they tell me it is even better than the mortar crew. I don't think I'll like it as well tho.

The lights went out about an hour ago and I am writing by flashlight so please excuse this mess. Someone blew a fuse. This is a pretty poor excuse for a letter but I know anything is appreciated at home and so I'll send it.

I love you all and think of you constantly.

Horace
