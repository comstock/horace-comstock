Here I am again. I got a letter from Dad and another from C.W. Haralson today. He was my primary instructor. I sure thought a lot of that guy.

Have I told you that we shoot skeet down here? It is to teach us to lead our targets and I have shot 8 rounds so far, seven of which were for record. My scores run 9 - 10 - 11 - 16 - 16 - 17 -17. Seventeen is an average scroe for a flying officer and I know
i can do much better in time. Most of the boys are getting around 10 - 12 now.
