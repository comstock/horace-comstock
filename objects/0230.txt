with some sort of problem during these hard days. The boys in the service wondering and waiting and dreading. The folks at the home waiting, praying and aching for their loved ones. The men in this theatre of opeations living in a sea of upheaval and their only relief is to fight and work hard enough so that they can't think. It is so damned big that you can't feel right to waste much time on your won petty, personal problems when there is so much to be done and so many problems so much bigger to be solved.

Regardless of all that men will think and wonder and worry about themselves and their loved ones.

As this letter would indicate my mind is a jumbled mess of mixed emotions. I have tried hard to place before you a clear picture of what travels through my mind.
