[June 1942]

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

Dear Dad.

I received your letter tonight and I don't mind telling you that I appreciate it a lot.

As for the money dont' get me wrong. I have spent less money in here than I have for a long time. At the times I wrote you I had $4.00 in my pocket. I lent a fellow $5.oo bringing my total to $9.00 that is what I had left out of $28.00 that I sarted with from Bangor. You see Uncle Sam doesn't start paying me untill the first of next month. Then I expect I'll get about $30.00. The following month I'll get $79.00. The reason for this is that I ahve already signed a $21.00 payroll and have some $9.00 allready due. The $50.00 per month, although it is effective as of June 1st will not start untill August 1st. It is all very complicated but that is the way it is.
