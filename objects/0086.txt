Incidentally while we are speaking of equipment and such if you really want to buy me a Christmas present that I can use and will be of real value to me buy me a bond!!! That's right a "War Bond." Uncle Sam provides me with everything I can use or need but it costs him money and he has to get from somewhere so that is the best Christmas present any soldier can get this Christmas. It is pretty hard to tell just where I'll be on Christmas day but if I know that you are thinking of me and that when you say Grace before you eat your Christmas dinner you asl God to watch over me and come for me -- my heart will be full of Christmas cheer and it will make it a lot easier wherever I am.
