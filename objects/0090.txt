Nov. 28, 1943

[Army Air Base
Tallahassee, FLA.]

Dear Mom.

I am still puzzled about that "corny" poem you sent me about mustaches. Would you by any chance be telling me that it didn't show? Perhaps it was a triffle weak. I'm really not the super sensitive type but I can take just soooo much. I tell you these guys just don't appreciate how hard I worked to get that thing to show at all. Then when I got it out where I thought it looked pretty good some silly refuge from a bush factory would politely ask me if I had washed my face yet and if I had not would I please do it. So I got tired of being the brunt of their quips so I shaved it off. I still swear if I had of had another week (or month) I could have waxed the end of it.
