I had planned to buy some things and send them home as late Christmas presents but the Arabs and French are making a fortune from the American soldiers and the quality of the merchandise isn't so hot any more either. I expect I can do better here at F.T.C. I'll know better in a couple or three days.

Well I have just about run out of gas so I'll send my love to one and all and as soon as I get straightened out here I'll try to get off some letters to all of the folks.

Loads of love
Horace

P.S. I sure woudl like to have some snapshots of everyone. (I mean some more)
