I. Campt any came through with flying colors as usual. 16 men out of 98. My section lost only 4 or so you see we had a pretty small percentage by comparison.

You will have received my airmail by the time you get this so please get that data lack as soon as possible if not allready on its way. You see I've decided to bring home a pair of silver wings in I can.

If I haven't allready told you there is no 176th it is the 167th. I don't know how I ever made a mistake like that. Regimental pride and all.

I'd give an awfull lot to see you all and that little girl of mine. I suppose however, I'll have to take it as it comes just the way we all do down here. Take good care of Elaine for me. She means an awful lot to me as you, no doubt know.

Your soldier boy in Texas
Horace

I love you all an awfull lot.
