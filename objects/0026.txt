March 11, 1943

[Pine Bluff School of Aviation
Army Air Forces Flying Training Detachment
Grider Field
Pine Bluff, Arkansas]

Dear Folks.

I suppose you're wondering what happened to me. Well at present there are 3 things standing between me and Basic School in Coffeyville, Kansas. No 1 the mark on my minimmum esential exams which I took this morning. No 2 one civilian check ride and No 3 just about 4 days time. I'll probably get my marks back tomorrow afternoon. I have my 60hrs and Mr. Haralson says I am a good flier. My marks have been fairly good but not as good as I had hoped. I believe I have an average of about 85%. It isn't bad but then I think I could do better. Most of this has been pretty dry and hard stuff to get interested in. They say that in Basic and Advance it is even harder but it is a great deal more
