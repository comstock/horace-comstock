April 24, 1943

[US Aviation Cadet Club
Coffeyville, Kansas]

Dear Folks

I know you must think I am a damn poor letter writer and I guess I am. I have a legit excuse for the past few days however. In the first place we had a shortage in stationary and everyone ran out. In the second place, I was hospitalized for 3 days. It wasn't anything serious but I learned a good lesson. You see I did some pretty high altitude flying and about a half hour later I ate a big mouthful of roast pork an as a result got a mild case of the Fliers Bends. I was pretty uncomfortable for a while. They did a good job on me and I feel fine now. I got a good nest and I think that was what I needed more than anything else. I have something interesting to tell you. Last monday I went to Link and spend an hour in a trainer when I got out the guy who ran it asked where I was from. I told him I was from Maine.
