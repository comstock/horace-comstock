1-21-1944

Dear Dad.

Beyond all shadow of a doubt "V" mail is the best form of correspondence. I just received your letter of 1-8-44 which is doggone good service. I also received my first communique concerning the addition to the Hawkins family. Somehow I knew it would be a boy but yours was the first verification I had had of the fact. Gee! just think what a time you folks will have when Trafton and I get back and start families! I expect my next stop will be the [???]. I am neither scared nor particularly enthused about the idea but after all it is the way you get back home again and that is what we are all working for.

Well news is scarce over here I guess I don't have much to chat about. Give my love to the kids and Grammy C. If you see that little wife of mine here her once for me and but good! Send Wilma's address and my best wishes and stuff to her.

Ain't this a mess!

Lots of love
Horace
