That is shop talk and I hate it. What do you hear from George? When is Wilma going to join him? I don't know that I have ever said much on that subject but generally when I start writing I am in such a mental muddle that anything I say is apt to be incoherenbt and jumbled.

I got a nice long letter from Grammy C. yesterday and answered it last night. She was terribly pleased because I wrote to her and I am very ashamed of myself for not having written sooner. I fear that after next Wed. my letter writing will suffer a lapse so don't be ashamed if you don't get any long letters for a while
