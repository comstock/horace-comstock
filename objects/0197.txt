8-18-42

so we get along pretty well. When we got back to Tiwoak Ranch where we hired the ponies the rancher gave him a collie pup and we brought him back to camp in a taxi. We named hem "El Diablo". That means the Devil and it sure fits. I guess I've written long enough so I'll sign off and send loads of love.

Horace

P.S. Did you ever get a letter from Elaine and forward it?
