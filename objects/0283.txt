As I was saying everyone from here to Kalamazoo has been calling to congradulate teh boys in 43-F and telegrams are as thick as grass. I have been chasing these new officers all over the post and have had the M.P. force helping me. It is a pretty nerve-wracking task especially when I could be spending a very pleasant weekend with my wife doing next to nothing. Elaine's address is just c/o Mrs. Clyde Mathis, Carrizo Springs, Texas. I don't know why I haven't written it to you before.

Well I have two weeks left and then I go to Matagorda for two weeks and then back here to graduate. I can hardly beleive it is true. All that is worrying me now is where they'll send me after graduation. I am praying for something on the east coast. I suppose that is out of the question but I can dream can't I?
