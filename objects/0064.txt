Oct. 14 1943

Dear Eulalie

How's the glamour girl? You probably think I'm a louse and I agree. I really should write much more often but I have so little to say that I get discouraged trying to write something of interest. That probably sounds crazy to you but the things that provide excitement and adventure and interest in my present life are mostly things I can't write about. You know what I mean like, for instance, gunnery scores and aerial flight tactics and just airplanes in general. Like the ship I fly now. I would tell you that when I take off I go scooting down the runway until I get 100 miles per hour and then come back on the stick and flip up my landing gear and go allmost straight up at the rate of 4,000 feet per minute.

I could also tell you that from point of take off I can take off and climb to 40,000 ft. in just 11 1/4 minutes. That is almost 8 miles
