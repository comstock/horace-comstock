My instructor is one Lt. Thomas from West Virgina and he is only 21. He seems like a swell fellow.

Do you know what I'd like to have? It is foolish but in another way very sensible. I'd like a pair of cowboy boots. They are prescribed footwear on this port and everyone wears them. They claim they are swell to fly in and the surrounding terrain makes it evident that they would be pretty swell to wear for everyday use. Low shoes are certainly tough. You have to stop every few minutes and empty the sand out of them.

There are no washouts here at Eagle Pass so I am at least sure of my wings.
