If my pay catches up with me next time she is going to too.

I got the $25.00 and believe me I need it. I had only .03 left when they got through deducting and I had bought soap, razors and tooth paste that I needed. My personnel officer told me that it would catch up with me within two months. By that time they'll owe me plenty, I hope. I hope to have enough to pay back the $25.00 and send for Elaine. It may be foolish and she may be a headache to me but I am convinced within myself that we have to find out for ourselves. If we can't make a go of it under trying conditions how can we in later life when we have  no difficulties other than the general run.

This is a punk letter, Dad, but I'll do better when I get to Pine Bluff.

Your son

Horace
