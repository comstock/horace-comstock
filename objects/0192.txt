See I told you so. I got called out at 11:45 and now it is 4:50. All afternoon I've been riding on a "Peep". Perhaps I should say I've been rideing **on** a Peep. I was anything but in it. We made 3 motorized attacks on an extrenched enemy field artillery position. I was in the first "Peep" in a column of about 50 "peeps", recon. cars and trucks, and troop transports. When you get a "peep" rolling at about 50 mph over the country roads of Okla. you can expect to get bounced around a little. You may have seen pictures of these little cars in full flight with all 4 wheels off the ground and take it from me it is not on uncommon occurance. In fact that is the way they look most of the time.

Oh yes, Elaine is in Jackson Heights NY. She is going to Washington D.C. to get a job (civil service). You'll never know what a relief it was to receive that letter, Dad. Women are funny animals I guess, huh? I've never received any payment from Hugh Tricky but am writing him this weekend.
