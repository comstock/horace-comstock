I am up here going to high school and so when I next I write.

My watch stopped a few days ago and I took it off and left in on my locker over night and the next morning it was going again. I think I had better have it cleaned.

Yesterday I got a nice letter from Woody and Ethel and also one from Beth and Myrtle. I have got to write to a lot of people and tell them of my change in rank.

Sunday I had a pretty good time. Cpl. Archer, a fellow
