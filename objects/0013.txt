This army flying is precision, precision, and more precision. When my instructor says, "Roll it out on the section lines or the highway or the railroad tracks," he means just that. All this flying that I am doing now is pretty swell but the great day is yet to come. That day is when I taxi out to the line and he hits me on the back and steps out and says, "Take her up, Mr. Comstock, she's all yours." It won't be long now either.
