Of course it is all pretty indefinite yet but I can see the pattern starting to take shape. After eight weeks of this type of training and eight weeks of maneuvers we'll be so tough and so fighting mad that we'll be itching to take in out on someone.

What the lieutenant started is over this morning. He said, "Boys, Mussolini has said that we're a bunch of creme puffs and pie eaters. I believe that we're going to surprise a lot of people. At least we're all Americans and that's half of the fight -- Come on you Yanks let's go." So we went. Some stuck their chings out and took it. Some didn't. I did and I'm going to keep it up if I possibly can.

You won't know me when I come home. I'll be as brown as a chocolate drop and I imagine I'll weigh at least 160# and it'll be good hard muscle. I don't know just what you'll think of this mess but I thing you'll be able to interpret my present mood.
