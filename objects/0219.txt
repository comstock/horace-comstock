However I'd like to clean up what I owe Dad and have it off my conscience. I have made one decision and that is to never lend any more money to my comrades-in-arms. The boys at Ft. Sill still owe me about $30.00 and I don't know whether I'll ever get it.

I suppose I should write to Capt. Castleman. He'd get if for me and someone would be in the dog-house but I don't need it that badly.

Well I guess that is enough of my troubles so I'll close for the time-being and send all my love to all of you.

Your pilot in Texas.

Horace
