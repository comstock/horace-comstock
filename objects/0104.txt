Feb 8, 1944

Dear Mother --

I have just finished writing Rene, my mother, Horace, and now is a good time to answer your letter of Jan 21, which I received yesterday.

From all I understand Peter is quite a boy. I was certainly glad to know that both mother and son were doing nicely. Rene wrote and said that you were both in Hartford at the same time. She seems to think that Peter is about the best thing that ever happened. She has her mind all made up about babies but I feel they are a very expensive luxury.

The weather over here has been very good lately but also very cold. New England is not the only place where it rains, snows, hot and cold all in the space of a week. Italy is about the same only we don't get quite cold or quite so much snow. Thank the Lord. Write soon. Did Bill ever get the flag I sent him?

Trafton
