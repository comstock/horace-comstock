July 8, 1942

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

You'll think I'm slacking up on my writing which may be so. It is however no fault of mine. These last weeks before maneuver are getting tougher and tougher. Yesterday we hiked 30 miles and tomorrow it is 36. It seems that we no sooner get into our tents than the whistle blows and we've got another 10 mile jaunt on our schedule.

Today we went 8 miles out into the Texas hills and dug fox holes and then came back si it wasn't too bad. Our goal is 120 miles in 3 days. That will give you and idea of what this Comando business is all about. I've been trying for three days to get a money order off to Elaine and some pictures printed for you and I haven't made any headway yet. It is pretty discouraging.
