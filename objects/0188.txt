with me when they get to it. Today I was called into G.H.Q. and given a task to do. Now I have a pretty good idea concerning my capacity. Alltho I'm not allowed to impart any information ever to you I can say it is highly specialized and have more power than I ever dreamed of. It sounds slightly forbidding I realize but alltho I may never gain any recognition through it I am proud to be chosen for the particular job. I was chosen out of almost 200 men.

This of course is strictly confidential. It might mean a great deal to me if it got out that I was engaged in this type of work.

I am really worried about Elaine. I got a letter from her mother and she's worried too. I wouldn't be surprised if she turned up here in Okla. That would be like the impetuous monkey. If you hear anything would you write me. Don't ever hesitate to talk to a lonely looking soldier, mother, its a swell feeling and lots of people do it. It helps to bolster morale too.

All my love

Horace

P.S. I got this all sealed and forgot to put in the negatives. I have tried several times to have some prints made to no avail so I'll send along the negatives and you can have ans many prints made as you like.
