March 18, 1943

[Pine Bluff School of Aviation
Army Air Forces Flying Training Detachment
Grider Field
Pine Bluff, Arkansas]

I got a long letter from you yesterday and for once I have plenty of time to answer it. Yesterday I completed my flying time. The day before I finished up in ground school. Now I have only thing more to find out before I am positive that I am going on to Basic. My minimum essential exam in engines is the key. Yesterday I had about an hour and a half to go on my flying time. The dispatcher informed me that I was scheduled to take my final check ride with one Mr. Haymore, although he's a nice enough fellow has earned the nickname of "Maytag."
