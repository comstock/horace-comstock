I rode with Mr. Haymore and gave him a take off, forced landing, 2 steep, vertical, 360 degree banks, 2 chandelles, some lazu eights, and a snap roll. When I had completed these maneuvers he said, "Let me have it, Mr. Comstock and I'll show you something." He sure did! He did Immellmans, slow rolls, loops, vertical reverses and half rolls all the way back to field. He then made me bring it in "hot" -- that is no flaps and under power. In other words I had to land it at about 125 mph rather than the usual 80 mph. He made 2 criticisms. No. 1, on one of my banks I used too much top aileron control and not enough rudder. No. 2, I waited too long to pick out my reference point for my lazy eight. "However, on the whole it was a pretty good ride," says he. This statement was followed by a dull thud. That was my carcase hitting the tarmac in a dead faint. No one believes me
