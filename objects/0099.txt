2-11-44

Dear Folks.

I have had another slight change in address. This new squadron is even better than the first. My location is a little differen also allthough I can't say it is any improvement. In fact I guess it is not so good at all. Incidentally, idi you ever try to take a bath in a tin helment? I am still trying to convince myself it can be done. These guys have been doing it for some time so I guess it is possible however after my first attempt I still look like a leopard spotted, you know. I have ceased to put on clean clothes and now I save my laundry bag for the least grimy! Aside from the problem of keeping myself in condition by digging elaborate fox holes. It seems to be a requirement wherever we go whether we need them or not. Fill in a new tent and dig a hole in the ground. Boy! when I get home again I am going to eat nothing but steak and chocolate fudge for a month. If anyone offers me a Vienna sausage or rice I am going to blow my top. It really isn't so bat but we have to gripe occaisionally.

Loads of love to all
Horace
