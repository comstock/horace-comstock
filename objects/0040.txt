From all they tell us our chances of seeing action in Europe are small. Our greatest menace at present is the Japanese [Niponese] and he is a more formidable foe thatn most people seem to think.

I have to turn my lights out tomorrow so I'll finish up tomorrow morning I hope.

Well here I go again. Since I have passed my code requirements and finished up in weather I have a little extra time in the morning. Yesterday I got quite a letter from Donald Danforth. He said that Purina's job was no longer one of the producing and selling grain but rather one of supplying the counties demands on her.
