Feb. 28, 1943

[Pine Bluff School of Aviation
Army Air Forces Flying Training Detachment
Grider Field
Pine Bluff, Arkansas]

Holy Mackerel! I don't ever intend to let a week go by without writing home. I may have slipped up but it doesn't seem possible. I know I didn't miss this week because this is the ?th letter I have written to you. The other morning I sat down and wrote until I was blue in the face. I am very well and happy in my work with the Air Corp. I have only 16hrs of flying time left here in Primary.

I got a surprise the other night that I won't forget for a while. I got a long-distance phone call from Blythewill Arkansas and who do you suppose it was--Earnie. He's going to the same twin engine advance school that Charlie Hutchins went to. We talked for about 20 minutes.
