I received Dad's $50.00 check and it is plentiful sufficient as the saying goes. As it looks now I'll have to buy a ticket to New York and come up to Maine from there. The chances are that I'll go directly back to Ft. Sam Houston Texas from there. That kind of messes up my chances of buying a round trip ticket but furlough rates are quite moderate so I'll not be much worse off. I wish there was some way I could come through Manchester without incurring additional expense but I won't know untill I can check on rates out of N.Y.

It is almost time for taps so I'll close this epistle for now and try to get another off soon.

Give my love to all and especially yourself.
