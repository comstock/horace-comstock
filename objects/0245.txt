The cadet byword is, "Eat, drink, and be merry for tomorrow you may suffer from chronic indigestion." It is a rather grim adoption of the real saying but it pictures the feeling of devil may care slam bangness of the cadet corp. We all know what is ahead and can't deny ourselves a push in that direction with usual feelings [of] mixed anticipation and dread. AS for fear, hell no, it doesn't exist. True we do dread it to some extent but feat has long since ceased to be a part of our nature.

As I reread this mess it seems to be a pretty drab excuse for a letter to you two but the way I feel right now it is the best I can do.

May God bless you both and keep you safe for each other in the world of freemen that must surely come.

Lots of love,

Horace
