In the past two weeks I have enrolled and taken care of all the needs of some 30 underclassman besides my regular duties as flight lieutenant. I am now studying Code, Physics, and Naval Identification. They are all tough subjects and take up most of my free time of which I have little enough.

I want to wish all of you a very happy and full Christmas and I sincerely wish I could share it with you. However there is some satisfaction in being a good cadet and a good cadet officer and good officer material for the U.S. Army.
