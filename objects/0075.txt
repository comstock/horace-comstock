I expect to be here for another 12 days and then off to Tallahasee and heaven knows what.

We are flying nights now so things are pretty dull during the day. Mostly we just sleep. Every now and then someone wakes up long enough to write a letter or two like I am doing now.

Gee I didn't mean to not write for so long but sometimes the time just gets away from us. No fooling there are times when I couldn't tell you what day it is without looking at a calendar.


Gee this is a lousy letter but at least it tells you that I am o.k. and how long I'll remain here what I look like. I've gotta go and check a schedule now so I'll say cherrio till next time.
