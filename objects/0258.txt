March 31, 1943

[U.S. Air Forces]

Dear Dad.

I got your swell letter and although I'm in no mood to write I am going to attempt to answer it. The reason for my mood, Dad, is because one of my buddies spun in from 200 feet today while doing pylon 8s. He and his instructor both killed. It is a pretty morbid place around here tonight. This fellow's name was L.G. Henry from Dallas Texas and he was really a swell egg. It kind of makes a fellow stop and think. Of course it isn't any worse that an automobile accident of the same caliber but it hits a little closer to home when you have slept next to the fellow for 10 weeks. Crack ups in training one few and far between so they make quite an impression. I expect that withing the next two weeks about 10 men will washout or quit on account of it. I have steeled myself against letting it make any difference in either my attitude or my flying. I know that my caution will be doubled but it should be the prominent factor in flying
