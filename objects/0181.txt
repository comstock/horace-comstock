July 22, 1942

[Fort Sill, Oklahoma]

Dear Mother.

I am now in Ft. Sill Okla. Boy do I get around. Our quarters here aren't so hot. (Tents) yet I am in the army and who am I to complain. The climate is however well worth the difference. It is hot but not the day still hot of Texas. We are near Lawton Okla. I am just 92 miles from Oklahoma City and hope to see it before I leave. I have seen Dallas Texas, Ft. Worth Texas and a few small towns of course I saw a lot of places coming down but they hardly count. I tried to put my Air Cadet through today and got stopped again. I am begining to think they want to keep me in the infantry. This time they say that we have to complete 3 months of training before I can apply for a transfer. Personally I think it is a lot of [???].
