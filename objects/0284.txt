6-26-43

[Army Air Forces Advanced Flying School
Eagle Pass, Texas]

So far we have covered allmost everything that we covered in Basic and are now getting aerial gunnery, straffing, and flight navigation. This is by far the most interesting phase of training. This business of Matagorda is going to be the real business. I understand we live under actual combat conditions, that is on a constant alert and are called upon to fly at any time or as long as they think we need it. However, when we're not flying we live the life of Riley. We lie around and get brown and swim and do just exactly what we want to allmost. I guess it has its drawbacks such as mosquitos big as horses and a few other little things but I guess I can stand that if it isn't any tougher than they say it is.
