Oct. 21, 1942

Dear Mother

I got your letter yesterday and this is the first spare minute I've had since then.

As I told you I am a flight lieutenant and wear 2 buttons on my shoulder. I have 60 men under my command and about 45 of them are fresh from civilian life. You can imagine the questions I have to answer daily or daily or can you? I get along very well with them and I think they like me and respect me. I also know that this business is very important to deciding whether or not you make second louy or junior warrant officer. I want to ge a lieutenant so I'm on the night tracks.

Elaine writes that she wants to come down here and I want her to as soon as we can make the necessary arrangements. If course I want to settle up with Dad before I undertake anything of this type. I signed the payroll.
