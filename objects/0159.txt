Tell Eulalie I'd appreciate a letter from her very much. There are two things that keep these boys going (that includes me) is mail from home and out loved ones and the other is the knowledge of what we're fighting for.

Really we're not having any picnic down here but we didn't expect that.

Sometimes whe I'm rolling around in the hot dust (and I do mean hot) and the tears and sweat are pouring out of me like a seive I feel like saying oh what the hell and quit trying, then I realize that this is all for my own benefit and if I quit trying that, as Sgt. McKinniny says, "It's your own little red cart." The more you put into it the better chance you have of coming out with a whole skin. It's worth every ounce of sweat you put into it. Some of the boys can't take it and they have to be carried off the field. I find it surprising just how much I really can take and not whimper. The food, incidently, is great and you can have all you can eat. Wait until I get up there and show you a real appetite.

So much love from your soldier in
Texas

Horace
