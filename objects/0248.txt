In ground school I am studying aircraft engines, theory of flight and aircraft identification. for four and a half weeks I'll be a lowerclassman and live in barracks then as an upperclassman. I'll move into a two man room. I haven't found out where they send their basic students as yet. I only hope they don't send us to Texas. As the song says, I've got too much Texas in my walk and talk. I don't want to go back to New York but I don't want to go back to Texas either.
