7-26-42

[USO]

Thursday I took out a "Class E" allotment. That is $25.00 per month will be coming home for you to keep for me. You see, Dad, when I get out of this mess I wan't something for Elaine and I to start on. It seems to be the general opinion around the army camps that the war will be over by Christmas 1943. I hope so but sometimes I wonder. I am on the inside and have seen this emince machine that Unc Sam is building adn believe me if it ever gets rolling it'll take some real doing to stop it.
