July 18, 1942

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

Dear Mom,

I haven't written anyone yet this week and am lucky to gram the time to write this letter. You see we're being moved to Oklahoma. My address for the next six weeks at least will be Ft. Sills Okla. Co. I 3rd Bn. 167th Infantry (R). The first three days of this week were spen on maneuvers in the Texas bad lands. Some fun! I went from Monday morning to Tuesday noon without food or water. I was on scout duty and roamed around by my lonesome doing what damage I could to the Blue Army without getting myself captured. It was kind of fascinating but not much fun. I'd rather eat ice cream anytime but then this
