I don't expect a furlough when I get my wings but I should be able to wrangle one after I them. I certainly hope I can. How's my sweet little wift getting along with my grand family? I sincerely hope she'll be with me very soon. I thought it would be all fixed up by now but I haven't heard from her in a week so I am kind of on the fence. It doesn't seem possible that I haven't seen her for seven months. Sometimes I feel like chucking this whole business and coming home to try and build a lifr for myself. That is of course impossible and I wouldn't even if I could that is not until the job is finished.

I must finish this up and run off a few lines to Elaine before I go back out to camp. So much love to the sweetest family any guy ever had.

Love
Horace

P.S. I'll honestly try to do better in my letter writing.
