That is to go to church on Sunday morning with my family. We soldiers get some of the funniest ideas like once when I was in the [rafautoy] and we were out on a field problem, I suddenly got a hankering for some pink chops. Well that [ainon] of thick fried pork chops was the most prominent thing in my mind for a week and when I finally got off duty and got into trying to talk sense to this crazy wife of mine for a half hour and she has one of her crazy streaks on and all she does is make me laugh. She's about due for another cold bath with her clothes on.
