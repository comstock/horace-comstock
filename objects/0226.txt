12-20-42

[San Antonio Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio, Texas]

Dear Mother

For the past two weeks time has been the most important possession I have had. I seriously doubt if I have written more than a couple of letters. I really don't know [if] I've lost track of all time and occurance. When I accepted this job I only had an inkling as to what I was letting myself in for.

I get your card and money and I'll put it to some good use if I even get a chance. As for me being lonely on Christmas I don't hardly believe I'll have time. I expect to go on guard as senior officer of the guard Wednesday night and I'll be onuntill 6:00 Friday night so I won't have time to ead hardly. I asked for it though so I can't complain. The upperclassman have Open Post Wed and I don't care
