12-11-42

[Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio, Texas]

Dear Mother,

I got your letter about 4 days ago and so I finally found a spare minute to answer it in.

Please, mother, don't ever think that anything you send me is a flop. You know damned well that a package from home is the sweetest thing a guy in the service can receive. Jut to get a package sends ones heart singing regardless of what it contains or the condition at the time of arrival. You package was in excellent shape and those chocolate cakes really hit the spot. I think they must have come in refridgeration because the box and everything in it was cold. Anytime you decide to repeat that process it will be perfectly o.k. with me. In fact I am looking foreward to another feed of those chocolate cakes.
