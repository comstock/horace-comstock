3-31-43

[U.S. Air Forces]

cocky little son of a gun. He is 2nd Lt. by the name of Lt. Welton B. Hodges. He stands about 5'2" and doesn't weigh more than 130#. He's blond and pretty good looking and can fly the pants off from anything with wings and a propellor. All four of his students are as tall or taller than I am. Two of them weigh around 180# and we all tower over him. He came through Randolph Field in peace time when a cadet had to have 2 years of college and know how to fly to boot. He's kind of sharp tongued and he doesn't waste any words in telling you what he thinks of you when you make a "buckethead mistake."

I have to study some navigaton now because I have a test tomorrow so I'll sign off. Tell Eulalie I'll write again soon and tell Billy I know I owe him a letter and I'll sho'nuff get it off as soon as I
