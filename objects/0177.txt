This platoon consists of about 200 men and many times they have to depend very heavily on that supporting fire for advance.

If I am forced to stay in the Infantry I couldn't have picked a better place myself. It is very interesting the sight on this mortar is so delicate that you can blast an orange at 100 yards. That is about as far as you can see one. The only other things in the infantry are rifleman and machine gun. Both of those are pretty dangerous.
