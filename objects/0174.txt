I got a letter from Dad today with Ray Dearborns letter of recommendation in it. I also got Steve's a few days agog so you see I am pretty well stocked. I find I can make an application for the "Air Cadet" exams after July 18. I expect I'll pass them O.K. I am studying on my own time and if possible I'll make and Airman yet.

I learned to day where Comnados their nam. The Comando is a small, vicious, black water snake that is deadly poisonous and noted for crawling up behind its prey and striking with unerving skill and disappearing.

This is a hurried mess but I know it will be welcome so I'll send you all my love and tell you that I am O.K. and getting tougher every day. I don't gain much weight but that that I have is hard as nails.

I love you all and pray for the day we'll be together again.

Horace
