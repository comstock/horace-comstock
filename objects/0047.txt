Pass is on the Rio Grande river. We have heard rumor that Cadets aren't allowed to have their wives with them there but all of them are going so I don't see what they can do about it. They claim that the housing problem is pretty acute.

If this is the case we are going to have the girls stay in Yuevaldy which is almost 31 miles from Eagle Pass.

I really feel quite honored to be sent to single. Only a selected bunch of almost 40 men out of 350 are going. My instructor told me that you had to be a good flier to make a good pursuit pilot adn he didn't think I would have any trouble. Praise, praise my aint I getting conceited.

Honestly though, I am only about 9 weeks from a pair of wings and that aint hay.
