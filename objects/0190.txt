I weighed myself today and weighed 150# and believe me that aint fat.

It has rained here some today but it was a welcome relief from the sweltering heat. The flies are becoming numerout and quite loathesome but that is nothing to a guy who has slet with black windows, tarantulas, scorpions and an occaisional rattlesnake. Ah yes I've seen armadillos too. Out here they are as common as mud turtles back home. A very queer animal. The armored car and tank units have them for mascots.

Last sunday I went to the Baptist Church in Lawton and really enjoyed the service. It was dedicated to the boys away from home so I guess I fitted the description pretty well. I'll close now and send you all my love.

Horace
