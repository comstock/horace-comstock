June 12 1942
Camp Bowie, Texas

Dear Dad.

This is pretty important so shoot it back as soon as possible if you will please.

I have got to have three references--character and adaptability and some money.

I found out about my "Air Cadet" today. You see I have to turn in to my Co. clerk these references and about 3 days hence. I'll get notice to report to a certain place for examination adn I have to pay my own expenses and 3 days lodgings. I also have to have another copy of my birth certificate.

If I can make the grade in the exam I can get out of this outfit and land in the "Air Corps." I'll be assigned a "Cadet" almost immediately adn left here untill they have room for me in the "Air School".

Please do this as quickly as possible and I'll appreciate it an awful lot.

Love
Horace
