May 17, 1943

[Basic Flying School
Coffeyville, Kansas]

Dear Folks,

Boy does it rain down here. It has rained almost continually for two weeks and hard too. Golley, you never saw anything like it. We had one morning that was fairly clear and we could fly.

I still don't know where I am going as yet just the usual amount of rumors with no definite origin. I am hoping against hope that I go to Victoria as it is the best bet by far of the possibilities. We are scheduled to leave next monday or one week from today.
