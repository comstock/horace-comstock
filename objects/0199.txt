girls in a new Chevy picked us up and brought us right to the camp gates. They were swell kids and it was pretty funny when we went by the slow-moving truck convoy. All the boys riding on hard wooden seats in the trucks and we went sailing by in a new car with girls.

Well tomorrow is the big day when I take my Air Cadet exams. I expect we'll go over at amout 7:30. From then on I must put my trust in God and hope for the best.

If I can make it I'll wear a pair of silver wings before I finish.

Gerad and Myrtle sent me a swell little photo album for my birthday and it really does bring back some very pleasant memories. I guess that is all the news from this end of the states.

Loads of love to all

Horace
