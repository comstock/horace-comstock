March 11, 1943


[Pine Bluff School of Aviation
Army Air Forces Flying Training Detachment
Grider Field
Pine Bluff, Arkansas]

Dear Mom,

Nine more days to go! Nine more days to go! 43-G is leaving Pine Bluff. So chant the remaining 175 boys of 43G to the time of Volga Boatmen, going to and comimg from classes. Each day the cut off another day. Yes since only 9 days left of Primary. I have now 57 of my 60 hours of flying time completed and am just as crazy about it as ever. What I can't figure out is what I am going to do with myself during those days between the end of Primary training and the beginning of Basic when I can't fly. We aren't flying today because of rain and heavy ground fog.

This past week has been pretty busy one. I have had 2 final exams and I have another monday. In my meteorology class I scooped an 89% and in Aircraft Identification I got a 94%. The exam in navigation will probably be a stinker and if I pass it I will be lucky.
