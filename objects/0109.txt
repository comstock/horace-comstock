Feb. 21, 1944

Dear Mom.

My mail is coming in spurts again. Yesterday I got about 4 bundles. It is fun to put them together and piece out what is happening back home. After about 6 letters I have a pretty good idea of all that has happened in the previous month. On the whole my mail is regular and quite plentiful at present. Things are pretty quiet now as we have been moved back with the rest of the groups.

The shell fire was pretty intense when we were up nearer the front. I guess we served our purpose and we are all glad to be back together again. You wanted to know what plane I was flying. I don't know whether the censors allow that kind of information or not but I mentioned it in previous letters so if some of my letters were cut up that is probably why. Believe me tho -- it is better even that what you called my "dream ship". It is truly the dream of any pilot to fly this wonderful fighter.

When I come home I'll tell you all about it. I don't think that day is so very far off either. Of all the foreign places I have been I wouldn't trade one square foot of U.S.A. for any part of it. So much love to you all.

Horace.
