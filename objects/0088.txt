Nov. 25, 1943

[Army Air Base
Waycross, Georgia]

Dear Dad.

This will probably be the last better home from here. Consequently I had better get myself straight and say what I have to first. To begin with Elaine has gone to Keene N.H. I think. I wrote to her to wire you or phone you as soon as she got my letter. You see when I get to my Point of Embarkation I may get a few days leave and if it is on the Atlantic seaboard I can get home. It would sure be hell if I came home and I didn't even know where Elaine was. This is Thursday (Thanksgiving Day) and I expect to leave Saturday. I have drawn all my equipment except a .45 pistol, a holster and an airplane. If I get the plane over here it means I am going to fly it over. That is what I want to do. Here is the rumor.
