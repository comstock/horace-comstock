June 9 1942
Camp Bowie, Texas

Dear Mother

Today they put the pressure on! They stepped the pace up to an 8 wk pace instead of 13 weeks. Believe me it is really tough. You pound yourself untill it seems impossible to go on and then get up and do it all over again. I don't know how long I can stand it but I do know when I go off the field they'll carry me off! They did carry off about 2 dozen today. It is surprising how much you can stand  when you really have to. A lot of they boys complain and blame our non-coms and say that they are just pounding us to pieces because we're Northerners and they're southerners. However, I know better. These non-coms took the same thing during their own training and theyh know that if we can stand it for 8 weeks we'll be able to stand almost anything. I know that I'm getting as hard as nails and I'm starting to put on weight. You see I figure it this way -- The tougher I can get and the more I can learn about high speed
