1-9-43

[Girder Field
Pine Bluff
Ark.]

Dear Dad.

I have a few minutes so I'll dart off few lines. To begin with I took my first solo flight on Sunday, the 7th and I now have a sum of 1 hr. 31 minutes of solo time. Out of the 315 men who came up here with me there are only 240 left and they are dwindling all the time. I expect to check out of traffic tomorrow or the next day and then I'll start piling up time. Mr. Haralson, my instructor, told me that I could have soloed at 8 hrs but he would rather keep me on dual until at least 9 1/2 hrs. I did solo at 9:35. The maximum is 12 hours. Believe me, Dad, that first solo flight was a sensation I'll never forget.
