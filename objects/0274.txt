June 19, 1943

[Army Air Forces Advanced Flying School
Eagle Pass, Texas]

Dear Mom.

That "ace" son of yours is on the rampage again. I don't know how long it takes my mail to get up there but I don't imagine it gets there in much less than a week. In a few days I'll have been here 4 weeks. Shortly after that I'll become an upperclassman and then I'll go to Matagorda and then a couple of weeks after that I'll graduate. I sincerely wish some of my family could be present when they pin those wings on me. However we all know how impossible that is so I might just as well wish for the moon.

I have decided in this first phase of my training that the Army has 3 ships that I want to fly. The Thunderbolt P-47, Lightning P-38, Mustang P51. Both the 38 and 47
