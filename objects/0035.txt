Gee, here it is almost a week later and I am just finishing this. A few days ago we mad our choice for single or twin engine Advance school. As you can guess I put my bid in for a single engine school. It is a well known fact that we will go back to Texas on either count. The singles will go to either Victoria Texas or Mission Texas, both are in the south east corner of Texas. I am allmost finished here. I have a few more hours of night flying and a check 9n formation flying and then I am all done.

It has rained steadily for 5 days here so we havn't done much flying. They are warming up the plant this morning so I guess we'll get in a little time today. I had an instrument check ride last week and I guess I passed it o.k. because no more was said. I have about 65 hours in Basic which makes me a total of about 130 hours in all. I'll probably have about 145 hrs when I leave here. In other words I can get about 15 hrs more here.

From what they tell us here we'd train in obsolete P.40's in Advance.
