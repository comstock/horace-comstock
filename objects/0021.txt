I don't know if I mentioned it or not but when we became upperclassmen we moved out of barracks into rooms for 2 men and it is much nicer. My studies, meterology and navigation, are much harder than they were as an underclassman. If it is possible I have less time than I did as an underclassman. I have about 35 hours of flying time now and about 3 hours of instrument time in a "Link" trainer. In about 3 weeks I'll be all done with my primary training (I hope).

So far I have passed a civilian "P"(progress) ride and an army "P" ride so if I can keep on at the rate I am going I'll make the grade o.k. If I am going to write 10 letters today I'll have to stop now.

All my love
Horace
