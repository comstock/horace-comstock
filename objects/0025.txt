In the two exams we have had so for in it I got 80% both times which is a bit lower than I'd like but you can't have everything. Navigation is really a tough subject and a lot of boys will be held over on account of it. If you have an average of 75% or less you have to attend night classes and so far I have been spared that. If you flunk the final you are automatically held over and that would break my heart. I am too set on flying a B.T. in the near future.

They say it gets more interesting in Basic and Advance. I don't see how that could be possible. I have never been anymore interested in anything in my life.

A furlough is something I have forgotten what the meaning of is. I don't imagine I'll get one until after all my training is finished and then I might not get one.

I don't have any idea where I'll go after Primary but it might be Waco, Tex.

I must close now and write to Elaine. Tell Bill I am going to write to him very soon and I think his letter was swell.

Love
Horace
