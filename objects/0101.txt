1-10-44

Dear Dad.

I got my first mail today since November 20th. You can't imagine how pleased I was. One from you and one from Elaine. Boy I was walking on air. Little did you know when you started you letter of 12-2-43 that it would travel halfway around the world to catch up with me. I didn't know it either. Believe it or not we have snow in N. Africa too. I am glad you and the boys get a chance to go hunting and I am also glad you got a deer. You did better than I did. I don't guess I'll be hunting any deer for a while. I wish it was deer. Perhaps "Tuffy" and I will get a chance to hunt together sometime in the future.

I still don't know whether "Wee" Hawkins is male or female and I am in allmost as much of a dither as I expect George was. I wrote to Wilma a while back and I expect a most interesting answer but soon.

Well, I am in fine shape and as happy as can be expected and am planning on getting a shot at a Maine deer myself next fall. God bless you all.

Horace.
