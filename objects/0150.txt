Every time he goes to the movies now I bump into him and vice versa. He lives in Burmingham Alabama. Yesterday he came down and hunted me up He said he had someone he wanted me to meet. It was his dad and mother. They were swell and wanted us to get a leave together and come up to their home. Of course I explained to that if I got a leave I'd be going to Maine but the gesture made me feel swell. That is typical of these southern people.

I am just begining to fidn out who I know around here. One fellow in my Co. comes from St. Johnsbury Vt. Howard Higgins, he lived uo near Phil Heath. Another Dick Moody lived on Massahesic St. in Manchester N.H. up near the Beacon Grain Store.
