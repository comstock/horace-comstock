6-5-42

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

It really is a small world after all. I sent Elaine a bunch of snapshots and I'll send you some just as soon as I can have them printed. Probably the first of next week.

I adt dinner up here in the Service Club today and for 68 cents I had quite a meal. Mashed potatoes, southern fried Boiled egg salad, creamed carrots, 2 bottles of milk -- corn bread, chocolate cake and ice cream. Not bad huh? There is plenty of it too.

I guess I'm pretty near sun down so I'll sign off and send you all my love. Take good care of a certain you know who and tell her I love her.

Your son
Horace
