May 4, 1943

[Basic Flying School
Coffeyville, Kansas]

Dear Mom.

I have a few minutes so I'll start a letter to you.

Elaine got here last Friday and we spent a perfectly glorious weekend together. We got a grand place to stay and we got it through the Cadet Club. It is with a mand and his wife and they have an adorable little boy. I slike an old maid but it is so damned swell that I can't help being a little effeiminate.

Mr. and Mrs. Elmore, that is thier name, are very religious babtists and he was in the infantry in the last war. They told us that they had been married for 18 years and been separated only 2 days in all that time so you can see they are a pretty grand couple.
