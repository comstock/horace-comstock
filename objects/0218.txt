11-7-42

[San Antonio Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio, Texas]

It won't be because I don't want to write but because Uncle Sam is really putting on the pressure and there is absolutely nothing I can do about it.

I will try to keep you informed as to my progress as much as possible but I know I'm not going to have time to write the way I want to.

Just how many allotments has Dad received? I am not unmindfull of the money I owe him but I have been payed only once since I got here and that was only a partial payment. I sent most of that to Elaine. Truly I have spent less money myself since I came down here than I have since I joined the Armed Forces of Uncle Sam.

I have neither the time or opportunity to spend much money down here.
