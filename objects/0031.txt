He was going to college while I was in Belfast but he knew Elaine and he used to chum around with both of Gram Cooper's boys and he knew all the kids that I knew. We had quite a session and he's really a swell egg. It seems prettyt nice to find someone who comes from your home town and you can really talk over things that are of common interest to the both of you.

The past week we have been flying cross-counyry and formations. Our cross country flights are about 150 to 200 miles long and it is really a lot of fun. Formation flying is even more fun. You can look across the few yards of space between you and your fellow flier and thumb your nose at them if you want to. I don't suppose that sounds like fun chat it really is quite a lot of fun.
