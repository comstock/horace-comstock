"Hey, Checker, Have you heard the one about the belly button?"

I, of course, replied, "No, what is it." The answer being, "Well you won't hear it 'cause it's a naval secret."

That's about the worst I've heard so you see there a pretty decent bunch of fellows.

I was really surprised at the way these southern boys accepted us. They have been swell and they don't seem to show any partiallity towards their southern brothers.

In closing let me say that I am sincerely proud to have such a grand guy to call Dad.

Your son
Horace
