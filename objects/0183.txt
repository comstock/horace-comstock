July 26, 1942

[USO]

Dear Dad.

Yesterday I got a weekend pass and some of us fellows come into Lawton Okla. IT is a surprisingly nice little town. It really isn't so little either. About the size of Manchester I believe. All the towns out here appear smaller because they are so flat you get in one tall building and you can see the whole town.

The USO club here is swell about a block of it. The people here are different from those in Texas
