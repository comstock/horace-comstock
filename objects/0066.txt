Nov. 1, 1943

[United States of America Air Forces
Dale Mabry Field
Tallahassee, Florida]

Dear Mom,

Yesterday I didn't to a doggone thing all day long. I slept and ate and slept some more. In the evening I went to the movies. You see we had overdrawn our quota of gasoline so we were forced to stop flying for a day. You can imagine how much flying we're doing to use that much gas. We are allowed some 70,000 gallons a day for about 25 airplanes. We have used as high as 40,000 gallons in a single morning. Now you know why gas is rationed. Consequently I haven't done much letter writting lately. Yesterday I tried all day to myself write but every time I'd allmost get up my ambition I'd fall asleep.
