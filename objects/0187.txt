Aug. 1, 1942

[Fort Sill Oklahoma]

Dear Mother.

I got a nice long letter from you today and it was very welcome. I also received a nice one from Grammy Beecher. That was swell of her and I'm going to answer it as soon as I finish this one.

Somehow I picked up a slight case of infection on the small toe of my left foot. It isn't serious but kind of painful to walk on. I went over to the infirmary today and they said they would lance it tomorrow so I won't get out of camp this weekend.

I got a check from Purina the other day and it is practically all gone now. No I haven't spent much. I have lent it to the boys. This camp is the most broke place about two days before pay day you ever saw. It is all perfectly safe because I've lent most of them money before and never lost a cent. They are all a pretty decent bunch of guys.

Up to now I've been in more or less of a fog as to just what they intended to do
