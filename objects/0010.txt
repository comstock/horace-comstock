1-15-43

[GROUP XIII
Army Air Force Flight School (Pilot)
San Antonio Aviation Cadet Center]

Dear Dad.

I got your letter today and as I haven't much to do for a change I can see no reason why I shouldn't answer it immediately. It is a very strange feeling to have time on my hands. I am awaiting shipment to Pine Bluff School of Aviation, Pine Bluff Arkansas. There I'll start my actual flight training. During the next 9 weeks I'll learn all the fundamentals of flying. From there I'll go to a basic school where I'll fly heavier and more powerful ships and from there be on to advance where my type of ship will be determined. When I receive my wings I'll probably be shipped somewhere under sealed orders. Then will start an intensive period of training in tactical maneuvers, gunnery, formation work and general combat principles. From there God only knows. That is the reason I am so keen on having Elaine join me.
