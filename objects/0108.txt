2-21-44

Dear Dad.

Not much I can write about over here but I can answer your letter. Truly I do get a thrill out of taking the world's finest fighter in the blue. Someday in the near future I'll tell you all about it. I have seen a little action and expect to see some more. On the whole "Jerry" is rather afraid of us. About the only time we him is when he's going away but fast. He doesn't much want to come to tangle with us because he always comes out th eloser by some considerable margin. The day will come when he will either have to stand and fight or quit altogether. I rather think the later will be the case. Italy is not so badly battered up yet that I can't appreciate the original beauty of it. The people, for the most part, are well meaning but ignorant and dirty. Africa is called the Ice Cream Front because alltho it is a theatre of operations still there is actually no fighting going on down there. The name indicates soft job, pretty good living conditions, WAC's and staff care. See what I mean?

Lots of love
Horace
