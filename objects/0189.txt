August 2, 1942

[Fort Sill
Oklahoma]

Dear Mother

You probably thought my last 2 letters quite skimpy, well they were. Mind you this one may not be any better. There are times when you just can't concentrate on what you want to write. At present I am pretty worried, as you know about Elaine. It has been almost 3 weeks since I last heard from her and I really don't know what to thing. You can imagine the things that run through my mind after taps, but then that is my own personal problem and it isn't hardly fair to worry you with it.

Today one of my bunk-mates moved out. He was a tall, good-natured redheaded egg named Haines. He came from Birmingham Alabama. He has been transferred to the kitchen to cook for us. I kind of hated to see him go and yet it is a promotion for him so it would be selfish to begrudge him the chance just for the loss of his pleasant company.
