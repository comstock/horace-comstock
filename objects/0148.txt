If you could see me now you'd not recognize me. I am turning the color of an over-ripe bananna and I have a regulation haircut. They treat us fine and we are all working very hard to please our superior officers.

Here is something that might be of interest. Today they don't train us to march--that is no much anyways. That is called close order drill adn we don't get much of it. Actually we get what is called column drill that is we spread out in squads of 12 about 15 yds from each other and

---

[page 3]

just walk along in a crouching position. Different had signals from squad leaders tell us what he wants us to do. Most of the time we duck and weave and squirm along grabbing whatever cover offers itself at the moment.

I'm in the toughest infantry outfit in the army or all the fighting forces baring none. The 169th is what was formerly the old Alabama 4th State Militia. If first commander was Stonewall Jackson. In the World War they fought hand in hand with the Fighting 69th of New York.
