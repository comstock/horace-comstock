Boy! am I behind on my coorespondence. The last week I was in Harris Neck. I was back and forth between Waycross and N.C. almost continually. We did our night flying out of Waycross. The mail just piled up. Today is the first day in a month I have drawn a free breath. I slept 13 hours last night and I am begining to feel like a human being again.
