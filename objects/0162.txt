June 11 1942
Camp Bowie, Texas

Dear Dad.

Firts I'll clear up a point on my address. It is 167th Infantry and don't let me tell you any different because I just went out and looked at the sign on G.H.Q.

I have a few things to tell you that I know will interest you. First thing is a Co. down here that they southerners call the "30 Day Wonders." That's us. Yes sir, it is Co. I - 167the Sup (R) 31st Division. Your son is a part of that Co. We are considered "crack" soldiers. Every time we go out to the drill field we pass in review before G.H.Q. and that is something that no other Co. does. Another thing is that yesterday we found out that we're bing trained as "Comandos." Ever heard of them? Well they're the creme of the crop.
