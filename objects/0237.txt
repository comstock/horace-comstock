and live for today and today only. I guess I fell into the way of thinking of most cadets. Their creed is, "Eat, Drink and be merry for tomorrow you may suffer from chronic indigestion." It is a pretty grim apostle of the original but summs up the general outlook of the ment who fly. A kind of grim shadow of humor over the reality of what is to come. You see pilot graduates no longer get furloughs when they complete their course they start combat training and aerial gunnery immediately.

I try so damned hard not to fall into this category but it is hard to stay clear of it. I don't want to let myself think of what is to come by I can't help myself. We all know what is ahead and what our chances are. The general attitude seems to be to grab what life you can while you have it to grab.
