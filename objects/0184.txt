Jimmy Cunny and Bob Englehart are the boys who came in with me. They are from Fall River Mass. and Providence RI respectively. As far as we can find out we came here to Ft. Sill to work under artillary fire. That is to say live shells will be bursting about 1000 yds from us. The gunners are old army men so it won't be so bad as it would if they were rookies.
