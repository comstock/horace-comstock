July 11, 1942

[Service Club
Camp Bowie, Texas]

Dear Dad.

I got you letter today and I'm really sorry if I upset you about that letter of reference. I find now that I can't take my exams until the last of this month anyhow. I have both Ray's and Steve's letters now. That was a swell one that Ray sent.

Yes I sent a letter to St. J. I figured it was the least I could do to show my appreciation for the swell bonuse check they sent me.

Here is a real bit of news. Today, Saturday the eleventh of July the Old Co. I joined the recruits. We're all regulars now. The weaklings have been weeded out and the cream remains. Guess what I got. Sam attached to
