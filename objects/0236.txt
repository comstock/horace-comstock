Dear Dad

I have already written to mother but I don't feel as if I had completed my job adn won't untill I have finished this and sent it to you.

To being with when I finihsed reading your letter there were tears in my eyes and I am proud of it. God knows there isn't a better dad under the sun and don't think I don't know it. I know you would back me ot the limit just as long you knew that I knew what I was doing. When I started talking about Elaine's coming down I thought I had narrowed my outlook down to the point that I was trying for. I find myself pitifully inadequate to meet the situation. I'd have given my right arm to have had her with me today and yet I know that I can't shut out the future
