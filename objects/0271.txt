They tell us that one good pilot is worth more than 10 good ships. I guess I'm pretty valuable, hug?

Last Sunda Elaine and I went to a little Baptist church over in Carny's Springs and we got a very warm welcome. The people made us feel very much at home. I guess we both felt better when we came out and strange though it may seem it was raining when we went in and the sun was shining when we came out.

Well I must write to Mr. Haralson so I'll close now and try to bet another letter off before they week is out.

So much love to all ofg you.

Horace.
