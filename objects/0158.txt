Yours, however gets here in about 3 days.

When we come in at nite we generally flop on our bunks and write a few letters and go to sleep. From Sat. at 12:00 until Monday at 6:00 our time is our own and there are several good post theatres and a library and Enlisted mens service club where there are all sorts of recreation -- ping-pong, pool, basketball and a cafeteria. There are churches of all denominations and we go regularly.

I spend half of yesterday afternoon (Sunday) writing and reading Shakespear's "Hamlet" in the library (pretty deep for me, huh?).

If this writing gets bad it is because my arm in begining to stiffen up.

I am really not planning to stay in the Infantry but then one never knows does one? After my eight weeks of preliminary are up I'll know a lot more about it than I do now.

I guess I'll be able to take the Flying Cadet test I hope.

I will try to have that picture taken next week up at the photo shop adn send it home to you. I'd like some snapshots of the family and that includes Elaine.

I'm sorry you couldent get dow to her graduatio. I know it would have pleased her.
