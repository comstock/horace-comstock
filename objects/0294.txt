July 30, 1943

Dear Folks,

I am writing from the "White Plaza" hotel in San Antonio, Texas. Today I am a second looey with wings of 1 1/2 days. This morning I got my orders to report on the 10th to Harding Field in Baton Rouge, La. Baton Rouge is the capital of La. It doesn't sound like too hard a deal. Now I am going to tell you what I have heard of this field. This is definately not for publication! I could be all wrong but I am hoping it is true. To begin with they have B-26 Martin Marauders, P-38 Lockheed Lightnings, and p-39 Bell Air Cobras at this field. The B-26 is a twin motored medium bomber adn is the fastest and most heavily armed plane of its type in the world. To fly one of these I would have to spend several months in a co-pilot seat
