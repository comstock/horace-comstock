That is the main type of drill. Close order isn't used so much. It is used for parade only. And there is very little time for parading.

Just accross the street is the section canteen. There isn't much there -- but about a mile away is the Post Exchange and they have everything from soup to nuts.

In my tent there are six other guys -- Eddie Barnes, Jimmy Curry, Fred Coats, leon Gorgan, Ralph Butts, Al Dill. They are all from Boston. The are a nice bunch of guys. Al has already sent home for his radio so that ought to be enough for one tent.

As for me bing a soldier from the first the one and only idea I had in coming into this fracass I guess that's the only thing to do.

I really don't mind the heat but sometimes it seems as if your insides were turned to water and leaking out through your pores.

The reason for my mail taking 6 days to get there is because it is free of postage and goes 4th class.
