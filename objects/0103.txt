Dec. 31, 1943

Dear Mom.

This is New Years Eve and as you can probably guess I am a little blue and lonesome. I am at the Red Cross club in town and I have to return to F.T.C. at 9:00. The boys are having a big blow out and I suppose the Scotch will flow pretty freely being New Year's and all. That is one of the things I haven't learned to enjoy as yet. I am a very light drinker. I think it saves me a lot of headaches.

You know I am really enjoying this business of seeing the world at the government's expense. We had it pretty rough in Casablanca but now we are back with the Air Corp and all living the life of a pilot.

As you see by my address I am still training. I don't know when they are going to give me a crack at the big boys. I am ready to try my hand when the time gets ripe.

I wish I had more time because I'd like to write to Lorene and Wilma but time is so doggone scarce. Perhaps tomorrow I can find time to do it without slighting my wife and family.

I must dash now if I am to catch my truck. Loads of love to my favorite family.

Horace
