12-11-42

[Aviation Cadet Center
San Antonio Texas]

to like it even better.

This week on Saturday we become upperclassman and I take over the duties of the Flight Lieutenant. All this is extra work but helps insure my commission. You see I don't want to be a Flight Officer I want to be a second lieutentant on flying pay. If I can make an officer I am going to try to pass my exams for a permanent commission. That will mean that after the war is over I can still retain my rating as a reserve commission. It is well worth having I think.

I got a nice long letter from Johnny Dore that I haven't had a chance to answer yet but I will tonight I think.
