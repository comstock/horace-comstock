I told you I'd clear $150 on my graduation but it won't be that much. We get it from what is left from our $250 clothing allowance and a month's cadet pay. My clothes cost me $177.45. My cadet pay will be about $65. However the following month I start drawing $327 per month. Then perhaps Elaine and I can live in some resemblance to a happily married couple not that we haven't had a lot of fun as it was but we had to count our pennies pretty carefully.
