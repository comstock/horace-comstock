2-7-44

Dear Mom.

How's every little thing at home? All o.k. here so far. There are some things I would sure like to write about but the censor would object so I'll have to be content with a few sundry comments about life in general. The weather up here in Italy isn't nearly as cold as S. Africa was. Perhaps it is the difference in altitude. We were pretty high up in Africa. Here it is like early fall in Maine and I really get the urge to go deer hunting but we are out for bigger game but the odds seem to be about the same. They do allow me to say I am a fighter pilot which you allready know. The most valuable asset a man has is a cool head in a pinch. I have found that I don't get excited very easily so I'll be o.k. I am still in the process of learning and allow I am equivalent to a ticket home for one of the old pilots so you can see they take darned good care of me. All my love to all and God bless you allways.

Horace.
